<VisuallyHidden asChild><h2>Intro</h2></VisuallyHidden>

<ComponentHeader title="PageHeader" tier="Molecule" />

The head of a page: an optional trail of breadcrumbs, the title as the page's heading, a description, a row of facts and the page's actions. A compound
component, made of named parts you compose: `PageHeader.Breadcrumbs`, `PageHeader.Title`, `PageHeader.Description`, `PageHeader.Meta` and
`PageHeader.Actions`, any of them optional. The first four stack in one column and `PageHeader.Actions` sits beside it, under it where there is no room
(a phone), so put the parts directly in the header.

`size` sets the title's and the description's text steps and the space around them (`md` is a page's usual head), and `bordered` draws a line under it. It is a
`<div>` (or a `<header>` with `as`) and adds no landmark and no tab stop. It is not navigation ([`Navbar`](/docs/organisms-navigation-navbar--docs),
`Tabs`) and not a card's header (`Card.Header`).

### Import

```tsx
import { PageHeader } from "@dbm-system/components";
```

## Usage guidelines

<div className="dbm-do-dont-grid">
<Callout tone="success" title="Do">

- Put the parts directly in `PageHeader`, in reading order. `PageHeader.Actions` has to be a direct child, so it can sit beside the text.
- Keep one `h1` per page: `PageHeader.Title` is `level` 1 by default. Give a section's header `level={2}` (or the level that fits).
- Put the page's own actions (the one a person came to do) in `PageHeader.Actions`, and keep them few.
- Put a status or who changed it and when in `PageHeader.Meta`, and the trail in `PageHeader.Breadcrumbs`.
- Give the title a `ref` and `tabIndex={-1}` if the app moves focus to it when a page's content changes.

</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger" title="Don't">

- Don't use it for navigation: tabs or a second row of links belong in `Tabs` or the `Navbar`, under the header.
- Don't wrap the parts in elements of your own: `PageHeader.Actions` inside a `div` is not a direct child and would sit in the text column.
- Don't use `as="header"` outside `main`: a `header` there is a second banner landmark. Use the default `div`.
- Don't put a card's title here: `Card.Header` is the head of a card.
- Don't put many actions in it: a page with a row of buttons wants a `Toolbar` under the header.

</Callout>
</div>

## Best practices

- Write a title that names the page, and a description that says what a person can do there, in a sentence.
- Use `size="sm"` for a panel's page and `lg` for a landing page; leave `md` for the rest.
- Use `bordered` when the content under the header has no surface of its own, so the header is told apart from it.
- A header has no tab stop of its own: the breadcrumbs and the actions are tabbed in reading order.

## Accessibility

<Callout tone="info" title="What the header says to assistive technology">

`PageHeader` adds no role and no landmark. The title is a real heading (`h1` by default), so it is the page's name and the place a screen-reader user
jumps to; the breadcrumbs keep their own `nav` and name; the actions are buttons in reading order.

</Callout>

| Key | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `Tab` | Moves through the breadcrumbs, then the actions, in reading order: the text column comes first in the page, and the actions follow it. |

- A `<header>` inside `main` is not a banner, so `as="header"` is safe there; outside `main` it would be a second one, so keep the default `div`.
- The title's contrast is the primary text colour on the page surface and the description's the secondary one (at least 4.5:1 in every theme); the header
  adds no colour pairing of its own.
- It keeps its order in right-to-left text: the text is at the right and the actions at the left.
- The title takes a `ref` and a `tabIndex`, so an app can send focus to it after a change that removes what had it.

## Code examples

```tsx
<PageHeader>
  <PageHeader.Title>Projects</PageHeader.Title>
  <PageHeader.Description>Everything your team is working on, in one place.</PageHeader.Description>
  <PageHeader.Actions>
    <Button>New project</Button>
  </PageHeader.Actions>
</PageHeader>
```

```tsx
// Native attributes in use: name the header for a screen reader, and let an app send focus to the title
<PageHeader aria-label="People">
  <PageHeader.Title ref={headingRef} tabIndex={-1}>
    People
  </PageHeader.Title>
</PageHeader>
```

```tsx
// The header of a section: a lower level, a smaller size and no banner inside main
<PageHeader as="header" size="sm">
  <PageHeader.Title level={2}>Members</PageHeader.Title>
  <PageHeader.Actions>
    <Button size="sm">Invite</Button>
  </PageHeader.Actions>
</PageHeader>
```
