<VisuallyHidden asChild><h2>Intro</h2></VisuallyHidden>

<ComponentHeader title="ToggleGroup" tier="Molecule" />

A set of options where the choice is shown: a segmented control. In a `single` group one item is chosen at a
time — a view (Day / Week / Month), an alignment; in a `multiple` group each item is its own on/off toggle — bold,
italic, underline. The items are fused into one control by default, or spaced apart with `attached={false}`, in a row
or a column, and the chosen one is marked in one of three looks (`subtle`, `outlined`, `solid`).

The group is one tab stop; the arrow keys move between its items (roving focus). In a single group they also choose the item they land on,
as in a native radio group; in a multiple group they only move focus, and `Space` or `Enter` chooses the focused one. A single group is a `radiogroup` of `radio` items, a multiple group a `toolbar` of toggle buttons. Inside a `Toolbar`, use `Toolbar.ToggleGroup`, which turns off the group's own roving focus (`rovingFocus={false}`) so the toolbar stays one tab stop; a multiple group is then a plain `group`.

### Import

```tsx
import { ToggleGroup } from "@dbm-system/components";
```

Its items are `ToggleGroup.Item`s.

## Properties

`ToggleGroup` also accepts any other native `<div>` attribute beyond what's listed here (e.g. `onFocus`,
`title`) — passed straight through to the underlying element. `role` and `data-orientation` are the group's own and can't be
overridden.

### ToggleGroup.Item properties

One choice in the group — a real `<button>`. It takes the group's `variant` and `size`; it has no say in either. Also
accepts any other native `<button>` attribute beyond what's listed here (e.g. `onFocus`, `title`) — passed straight
through. `role` and the checked or pressed state are the item's own and can't be overridden.

## Variants

### Every look

`outlined` (the default) gives every item a border and marks the chosen one with the brand colour, so nothing moves
when the choice changes. `subtle` has no borders and tints the chosen item. `solid` keeps the borders and fills the
chosen item with the brand colour.

## Behaviour

### Deselectable

A single group keeps one item chosen once it has one: clicking the chosen item does nothing, as with radio buttons.
`deselectable` lets that click clear it, so the group can have no choice (a filter that can be off).

### Right to left

Set `dir="rtl"` on the group: the first item is at the right, the outer corners mirror with it, and the arrow keys
follow the reading direction. Left out, it follows the nearest `DirectionProvider`, else left-to-right; it is not read from the page.

## Usage guidelines

<div className="dbm-do-dont-grid">
<Callout tone="success" title="Do">

- Use a `single` group for a few mutually exclusive options that switch a view or a setting at once, and a `multiple`
  group for independent on/off options.
- Give the group a name (`aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby` pointing at a visible label).
- Keep labels short and parallel ("Day", "Week", "Month"); use icon-only items only for familiar icons, each with an
  `aria-label`, and a `Tooltip` where the icon alone is not enough.
- Use `deselectable` when "none chosen" is a valid state, such as a filter.
- Stack a long group on a phone with an `orientation` map rather than letting it overflow.

</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger" title="Don't">

- Don't use it to run actions. A set of buttons that each do something is a `ButtonGroup`.
- Don't use it to move between views of a page: that is `Tabs`, which owns the panels and their announcement.
- Don't put more than a handful of items in an attached row: it can't wrap. Stack it, or space it.
- Don't use it for a long list of options, or one that grows: a `Select` or a `RadioGroup` scales better.
- Don't rely on colour alone to show the choice in your own styling: the group's looks also change border or fill, and
  the state is exposed to assistive tech as `aria-checked` or `aria-pressed`.

</Callout>
</div>

## Best practices

- Prefer `outlined` when layout stability matters: every item already has a border, so choosing one changes colour only.
- Keep to two to five items. Past that, a `Select` reads better.
- In a full-width group every item gets the same width, so one long label squeezes the rest.
- Reach for `rounded` when the surrounding controls are pills too; leave it off when they are square-cornered.
- Pick `single` when exactly one option applies, even if it may be cleared, and `multiple` when any combination
  makes sense.

## Accessibility

<Callout tone="info" title="A radiogroup, or a toolbar of toggles">

- A `single` group renders `role="radiogroup"` with `role="radio"` items (`aria-checked`); a `multiple` group renders
  `role="toolbar"` with toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`). Either is named by `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`; a
  dev-mode console warning fires once if it has neither, and once per icon-only item without a name.
- The group is one tab stop. `Tab` enters it on the chosen item (or the first), and the arrow keys, `Home` and `End`
  move focus between the items. In a single group focus and choice move together, as in a native radio group (so
  tabbing in, or clicking away and back, chooses nothing); in a multiple group they only move focus, and `Space`
  or `Enter` chooses. The arrows follow the reading direction,
  so `dir="rtl"` swaps them, and `loop` decides whether they wrap.
- A disabled item is skipped by the arrows and announced as unavailable.
- The chosen item is marked by more than colour where the look allows it: a brand border on `outlined`, a fill on
  `solid`, and the exposed state on all three.
- Items are at least 24 × 24px (WCAG 2.5.8) at every size and in both orientations, including icon-only ones.
- When attached, the focus ring is drawn inside the item so the next item never hides it, in a colour that reads on a
  filled item.
- The corners and shared borders are logical, so `dir="rtl"` mirrors them without any other change.

</Callout>

```tsx
  <ToggleGroup.Item value="month">Month</ToggleGroup.Item>
</ToggleGroup>

<ToggleGroup aria-label="Text style" type="multiple" size="sm">
  <ToggleGroup.Item value="bold" icon={TextBIcon} aria-label="Bold" />
  <ToggleGroup.Item value="italic" icon={TextItalicIcon} aria-label="Italic" />

// Controlled
const [view, setView] = useState("week");
<ToggleGroup aria-label="View" value={view} onValueChange={setView}>…</ToggleGroup>

<ToggleGroup aria-label="Filter" deselectable>…</ToggleGroup>
<ToggleGroup aria-label="View" variant="solid" rounded fullWidth>…</ToggleGroup>
<ToggleGroup aria-label="Filter" attached={false} type="multiple">…</ToggleGroup>
<ToggleGroup
  aria-label="View"
  orientation={{ base: "vertical", md: "horizontal" }}
>
  …
</ToggleGroup>
<ToggleGroup aria-label="Alignment" dir="rtl">…</ToggleGroup>

<ToggleGroup aria-label="View" onFocus={() => trackFocus("view")}>…</ToggleGroup>
```
