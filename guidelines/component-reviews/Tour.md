# Tour — review findings

**Status:** Built 2026-11-01 with the `06-engineering-standards.md` §9 checklist applied as it was written. **Not Finalized**: only the maintainer declares that. The items under "Not verified" are open.

## What was built

A guided tour of a page already on screen. `steps` is data (`id`, `target`, `title`, `content`, and per step `side`, `align`, `padding`, `interactive`, `advanceOn`, `onEnter`, `missingTarget`, `media`, `footer`, `actions` and `labels`); each step dims the page except one element and puts a card beside it with "Step n of N" and a small Skip tour (with a "more" button opening the dismissals) on the progress row, and Back and Next or Finish below. The step showing is `value` (an id), controlled or not; the tour opens from `open`/`defaultOpen` or `controllerRef` and tells `onOpenChange` why it ended. A step with no element, or whose element never appears (or is skipped, with `missingTarget`), is a centred card. `presentation` is `anchored` or `docked` (a breakpoint map works), `progress` is `text`, `bar`, `both` or `none`, `dismissals` and `dismissalsOn` add "Remind me later" and "Don't show again", `scrollOffset` clears a sticky header, and it takes `scrimOpacity`, `closeOnOutsidePress`, `targetTimeout`, `onTargetMissing`, `container`, `dir`, `labels` and `formatNumber`. It runs inside a modal `Dialog` when rendered in its content.

## Decisions
- Prop-driven over a `steps` array, an id as the value, no trigger, "seen once" a recipe on `usePersistentDismiss`: [ADR-0165](../adr/0165-tour-is-a-prop-driven-overlay-over-a-steps-array-with-a-step-id-as-its-value-and-no-trigger-over-a-compound-of-step-parts-or-a-built-in-start-button.md).
- A ring with a shadow dim, a shield with a `clip-path` hole for an `interactive` step, a non-modal dialog holding focus, the ring read every frame while it moves and then every quarter second: [ADR-0166](../adr/0166-tour-dims-the-page-with-a-moving-ring-and-blocks-it-with-a-shield-and-keeps-focus-in-a-non-modal-card-over-a-modal-dialog-an-svg-mask-or-four-panels.md).
- Radix Popper directly (pinned 1.3.7, the copy Popover already uses) and a docked presentation: [ADR-0167](../adr/0167-tour-positions-its-card-with-radix-popper-over-popover-and-docks-it-across-the-bottom-on-a-phone-over-a-popover-collision-shift.md). This differs from the plan's Radix Popover: Popover's content forces its own `role="dialog"` and a looping focus scope, which an `interactive` step cannot turn off.
- **There are no progress dots from `Indicators`:** it is always a row of buttons, and a tour's progress is not something to press. The progress bar (`progress="bar"`) is the tour's own segments.
- No new token. The padding is a step on the spacing scale read as `--dbm-space-*`; the card is `Popover`'s surface; the dim is `bg.overlay` at `opacity.60`. The progress bar adds two pairings against the card, checked in all four themes (see the final review): a reached segment is `bg.brand` (6.1:1 to 8.5:1) and one not reached is hollow in `border.strong` (6.9:1 to 10.5:1).
- Bundle: 14.8KB of JS and 4.6KB of CSS with the dismissals menu, a 16KB budget (8.9KB and 2.5KB without the menu).
- `internal/useReducedMotion.ts` (a store with a server snapshot) is new shared code.

## What was found while building it
- **A card drawn afresh for each step loses the focus that was on it.** The next step's element is looked up in a frame, so the card is removed and drawn again, and the control that was pressed is gone with it. The pressed control is remembered by name (`data-tour-action`) and found again in the new card; the first version cleared the note against the old, detached card, and the card then ended up focused instead. A second remount came from reading a stale box for the new step as the middle of the screen, drawing a centred card first; a box now names the element it was measured for, and the card waits for its own.
- **A page that is not visible runs no animation frames,** so a tour opened in a hidden tab (or a preview pane that is not shown) sits empty until the tab is shown. The tour is a visible-page feature, and the browser tests run on a shown page.
- **A tour drawn into a flex box adds its own gap.** The layer is a child of its container, so a flex column with a `gap` grows by one gap while the tour is open and the items under `margin-top: auto` move up. The story kit's "behind" button is absolutely placed for that reason, and the first check failed on it.
- **Fixed positioning inside a container is measured from the container's corner,** not the viewport's: the ring and the card are in the same containing block, and positions are read from a hidden probe that covers it. The first check measures the ring against its element inside a transformed container.
- **Popper reads the ring, not the element,** so the card's offset includes the padding and it follows the ring as it eases (`updatePositionStrategy="always"`).
- **Hit-testing follows `clip-path`,** which is what lets a press through the hole of an `interactive` step; the check presses the field for real and types into it, and presses the page outside the hole and sees the count stay at 0. A shield removed would fail it.
- **The first story's first check was flaky until the web font had loaded** (the field's height changed after it was measured): every check waits for `document.fonts.ready` and for the ring to be still for a few frames.
- Radix's own focus scope pauses an outer scope when another is mounted, so the trap is `FocusTrap`'s, put on the card itself, and the initial focus is the card (so the dialog and its name are read), not its first button.

## Additions (2026-11-01, at direction)
Eleven gaps found after the first pass, built the same day. Decisions: [ADR-0168](../adr/0168-tour-runs-inside-a-modal-dialog-by-being-rendered-in-its-content-above-its-panel-and-keeping-escape-to-itself-over-a-layer-manager-or-leaving-dialogs-unsupported.md) (dialogs) and [ADR-0169](../adr/0169-tour-steps-can-advance-on-their-elements-events-run-a-hook-before-they-are-shown-skip-a-missing-element-and-offer-later-and-never-over-leaving-each-to-the-app.md) (step events, the entry hook, a missing element, dismissals, richer cards, `scrollOffset`); ADR-0166 and ADR-0167 were amended in the detail.
- **Focus across a change of form.** The card is drawn afresh when `presentation` changes between anchored and docked. Whether focus was inside the card, and on which control, is remembered by name; when the old card goes and focus is on `body`, the control (or the card) is focused in the new one. A real-browser check swaps the form with Next focused, there and back.
- **Tab on an interactive step** runs round the card and the element's own controls in both directions. Two handlers saw the same press (the card's moved focus, then the page's moved it back), so the second ignores a press already handled.
- **A tour inside a modal `Dialog`** works when rendered inside `Dialog.Content`: the dim and shield moved up to the `modal` step, they and the card say `pointer-events: auto`, and Escape is heard on `window` in the capture phase and stopped. The check presses the card for real and sees the dialog stay, then Escape closes the tour and the next one the dialog.
- **A docked card clears a home indicator** (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) **and an on-screen keyboard** (`--tour-keyboard-inset`, from the visual viewport, in `useKeyboardInset`).
- **Measuring is idle when nothing moves:** a frame at a time while the box changes, then every 250ms, waking at once on a scroll, a resize or a change in the element's size. The first attempt switched Popper's `updatePositionStrategy` to follow it, but that is fixed when the card first mounts, so a card mounted while moving kept asking for a frame for ever; the ring is a real element, so Popper's own move detection follows it and the strategy is left alone. The check counts the frames the tour asks for over 700ms (under 8, against about 40) and then scrolls and sees it follow. A page mutation (content inserted or restyled), a transition or animation starting or ending, a scroll, a resize and a change of the element's size each wake it at once; checks insert content above the element with no scroll (the spotlight follows within 400ms) and move the element with a CSS animation (it stays on it), and sample the card against the ring through a step change (it stays within 40px of its resting gap, and ends at it exactly).
- **The dismissals took six layouts.** In the main row beside Skip, on a line of their own, in a footer strip side by side, behind a "more" icon button in the title row, in a footer again (stacked, then one centred row, with trailing carets), and now behind a "more" icon button right after Skip. Skip is a small (`xs`) button at the end of the progress row ("Step 2 of 4") on every step but the last, so the main row holds only Back and Next, and the button is the same size as Skip, in the brand tone (its icon is the same colour as Skip's text). The cost of the menu is real: it is the `Menu`, so Tour's bundle is 14.8KB of JS (budget 16KB, in `check-component-bundle-size.mjs`), and the two focus scopes of the card's `FocusTrap` and the menu fought over focus (in jsdom they recursed until the stack overflowed and a test run spun at full CPU), so the card's trap stands down while the menu is open (`onMenuOpenChange`), Escape is not taken by the tour while it is open, and the card's key and focus handlers ignore anything that reaches them from the portaled menu. A real-browser check measures the button against Skip, presses it for real, sees focus move into the menu, Escape close only the menu and focus return to the button, and an item end the tour with its reason; the card is hidden from the accessibility tree while the modal menu is open. `dismissalsOn="first"` shows the button on the first step only.
- **The ring eased all the time, so it trailed a moving element** by the transition's duration (18px behind a CSS animation, and a rubber band behind a scroll). It now eases only for a moment after the step changes (`data-easing`), and follows its element with no easing otherwise.
- **`advanceOn`, `onEnter`, `missingTarget`** (each per step; `missingTarget` also on the tour), **`dismissals`** (`later`, `never`), **`scrollOffset`**, **step `media`, `footer` and `actions`**, and **`progress="bar"` and `"both"`**, in ADR-0169. `onEnter` runs twice under `StrictMode` in development.
- A `Tab` target check had to use computed `display` and `visibility`, not `offsetParent` or `checkVisibility()`, which read a card in jsdom as hidden.
- The SSR test unstubs the setup's `ResizeObserver` with the other globals, so it stubs its own.

## Final review (2026-11-02)
A full §9 pass before Finalizing found eight things, all fixed:
- **The progress bar's unreached segments were nearly invisible.** `bg.neutral-subtle` on the card measured 1.04:1 in both light themes and 1.40:1 in both dark ones, so with `progress="bar"` the steps still to come could not be seen, and reached against unreached was colour alone. They are now hollow, outlined in `border.strong` (6.9:1 to 10.5:1 on the card), and reached is filled `bg.brand`; a check reads the computed colours and asserts 3:1 for both and that an unreached one has no fill, and fails with the old colour.
- **A caller's `onKeyDown`, `onFocus` and `onBlur` were dropped,** because the card's own were set after the spread. They are composed (the caller's first; a handler that claims a key keeps it), and tests pass one of each.
- **`actions` said it replaced Skip, Back and Next,** but Skip lives in the progress row and stays. The Docs and types now say "in place of Back and Next (Skip stays)".
- **`title` is not a prop** (it is omitted from the native attributes, since it would be read as a step's title) yet the Docs listed it and an example passed it. The sentence and the example use `lang` and `data-*`; a throwaway typecheck of every snippet, both Playground variants and every Docs code example, with a planted bad prop to prove it bites, found nothing else.
- **The Playground had controls that did nothing** (`missingTarget`, `targetTimeout`, `dismissalsOn`, and `dismissals` had no control). Two Playground-only switches (`showDismissals`, `missingStep`) now set the page up, and the dependent controls appear only once they are on (`if`); the panel was driven live, and the dismissals menu appears.
- **No check pinned a phone width.** A story pinned to `mobile1` asserts the viewport is under the breakpoint, then that `{ base: "docked", md: "anchored" }` docks the card across the bottom with the element clear of it; a desktop twin asserts it anchors. Both fail when the map is broken.
- **Every shown story is on the Docs page.** A test lists the stories not tagged `!dev` in the stories file and fails if any lacks a `<Canvas>` in the Docs page; all 15 are there.
- This file's counts and its "no new colour pairing" line were stale and are updated.

## Verified
- 114 unit checks (structure, steps, controlled and uncontrolled, the controller, every target form, a missing and an invalid target, focus and announcements including `StrictMode`, labels, docked and interactive, the additions above, axe), a server render and hydration check, and the Docs page's token table against the stylesheet.
- 47 real-browser checks, run repeatedly with no failure: the ring against its element inside a transformed container; the card clear of the ring and above the shield; real presses on the dimmed page, through an `interactive` hole and on Next; Tab and Shift+Tab loops; focus after a keyboard step and Back; Escape and the focus it returns; a centred card; a docked card; a real wheel on the page and a scrolling ancestor; right-to-left side and arrow keys; the container's edge; focus across a change of form; Tab round an interactive element; `advanceOn` by a real press; idle measuring; a sticky header; a tour inside a dialog; the progress bar in forced colours and its segments' contrast; a phone-width story and its desktop twin for the responsive map; forced colours and reduced motion (each cleared with `features: []`); the 24px target size; axe on every story.
- The Docs page was opened in a running Storybook: nine canvases, no error, the headings of the template, the Properties and step tables. The docked card was looked at in dark mode.

## Not verified
- **A screen reader.** The card's name, description, the announcement of a step and the loop were checked by attributes and by axe, not by listening.
- **All four themes.** The Purple theme in light and dark was looked at; Emerald was not (the progress bar's contrast was measured in all four).
- **Right-to-left `align`.** Only `side` is checked in right-to-left; `align: "start"` there was not confirmed to put the card's start edge on the right.
- **A real phone.** The docked card is checked at a pinned phone width (`mobile1`) in a desktop browser, with no touch, no on-screen keyboard and no home indicator. The keyboard lift is checked in jsdom with a stubbed visual viewport and the safe-area inset by its CSS only; neither was seen on a device.
- **A step whose element moves on its own** (an animation) is followed frame by frame, and was not stress-tested; a very long page with many steps was not either.
- **A tour rendered beside a dialog** (outside its React tree) still dismisses it on a press; documented, not changed.
- **`onEnter` that never settles** holds the card for ever; there is no timeout.
- The Docs page shows an `h2` (the card's title) while a demo is open, which a table of contents built live would list for as long as it is open.
