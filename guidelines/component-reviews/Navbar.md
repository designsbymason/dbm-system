# Navbar — review findings

Eleventh organism, built 2026-10-17 with the `06-engineering-standards.md` §9 checklist applied as it was written. **Finalized 2026-10-19**, at the maintainer's declaration after the review rounds below and a final documentation pass (the Docs page, prop descriptions and snippets checked against the code and each story's bar), with the items under "Open at Finalization" accepted as open: none changes the component.

## Decisions
- Collapses by a CSS breakpoint and draws its links in the bar and in the menu panel, with plain tab order: [ADR-0091](../adr/0091-navbar-collapses-by-a-css-breakpoint-and-draws-its-links-in-the-bar-and-in-the-menu-panel-over-measuring-its-width-or-writing-two-lists.md).
- Compound: `Navbar` (a `<header>`), `Navbar.Brand`, `Navbar.Links` (a named `<nav>` holding a `role="list"` list), `Navbar.Link`, `Navbar.Group`, `Navbar.Actions`, `Navbar.MenuButton`. The phone panel is `Drawer`, the button an `IconButton`, `sticky` is `Affix` (the same `sticky`/`stickyOffset`/`scrollContainerRef` as `Toolbar` and `Alert`), `contained` is `Container`.
- `variant` (`outlined`, `filled`, `ghost`), not `surface`: `05` §2 reserves `surface` for a group of controls whose `variant` already names the controls' look, and a bar's links aren't `Button`s.
- **No new token.** The bar's height is the `icon-button.size.*` token of its step plus `space.1` or `space.2` above and below, so `03`'s order of preference (an existing scale first) holds. The current link's colours (`text.brand` on `bg.brand-subtle`) are the pairing `Tabs` uses.
- `Navbar.Link` draws its own `<a>` (and `Slot` for `asChild`) rather than composing the `Link` atom: `Link`'s look is a text link in flowing prose, and overriding its colour, underline and shape from outside would depend on stylesheet order. It reproduces `Link`'s disabled handling (`aria-disabled`, a capture-phase click guard, stays focusable).
- `NavigationMenu` stays a separate 🟡 component for mega-menu panels. A dropdown in the navigation is a `Navbar.Group`, so `Navbar` alone covers v1, which is the decision `04` left open for this component.
- `Navbar.Group`: a menu in the bar and an expandable group in the panel, and a press closes the panel only on a link: [ADR-0092](../adr/0092-navbar-group-is-a-menu-in-the-bar-and-an-expandable-group-in-the-menu-panel-over-a-floating-list-everywhere-or-a-mega-menu.md). Replaces the first version's recipe for a dropdown.
- Not built, additive later: a brand-coloured `tone`, collapse by measured width, hide-on-scroll-down.

## What was found while building it
- **A router's link never closed the panel.** The first version skipped closing when the click's default was prevented, and a client-side router prevents it to navigate without a page load, which is exactly the link that most needs to close the panel. Found by writing the story's interaction test with a link that prevents its click; it now closes whatever the click's default.
- **A link in a hidden bar has no accessible name in jsdom or in a browser.** jsdom applies the stylesheet but matches no media query, so below the step the bar's links are `display: none` and a name query finds nothing; the unit tests read them with `hidden: true` and the attribute, and tests that need them on show use `collapseBelow="never"`.
- **The brand was an 18px target** (found by the 24px check at every size); it has a `min-block-size` of `space.6`.
- **Several bars in one gallery are several banner landmarks,** which axe rejects. A gallery draws each bar in a named `<section>`, which makes its `<header>` an ordinary region (the Docs page says the same for a page with more than one).
- **A story that clicks a `#hash` link closes the test page** in the browser runner; the interaction test stands in for a router by preventing the click.

## Second pass: dropdowns in the navigation (2026-10-17)
- **The recipe I first wrote for a dropdown (a `Menu` whose trigger is a `Navbar.Link`) was untested and wrong in the panel:** every press of a link-styled control closed the panel, so the trigger closed it as the menu opened. A press now closes it only on an `<a href>`, and `Navbar.Group` is the real part.
- **Found by the checks:** a native `disabled` button inside `Menu.Trigger asChild` still opened the menu on a pointer press, so `disabled` goes to the trigger too. The click-guard test was proved to bite by removing the guard and watching it fail.
- **Checks added:** 9 unit tests (the click rule, the menu's items, current mark and disabled item, the arrow key, focus order and return, a disabled group, the button's ref and attributes, the panel disclosure and `aria-controls`, starting expanded for the current page, nested list roles, jest-axe closed and open) and 2 real-browser checks (a group opening above a sticky bar with every item the top thing at its centre and 24px or more, drawn at the height and colour of its sibling links, with focus back on the button; and the panel group collapsed to no room, expanding indented beneath its button without moving the panel, with a chosen link closing it).
- **Not verified:** a real finger on the panel disclosure, and a group's menu in right-to-left text beyond `Menu`'s own checks.

## Third pass: how the current link is marked (2026-10-17)
- **`currentVariant`** (`underline` default since the fourth pass, `subtle`, `solid`; the names `Tabs` uses), one look for the whole bar, the phone panel and a group that holds the page, with no per-link override. The class is put on the links themselves and not on the root, since the panel is drawn in a portal outside the bar.
- **No new token and no new pairing to invent:** `subtle` is `Tabs`' tint, `solid` is a primary `Button`'s `text.on-brand` on `bg.brand` with the ring drawn inside the link in `icon.on-brand` (`05` §6), and `underline` keeps `text.primary` with a `bg.brand` line. In the panel the underline is a 2px bar at the start edge, since a bottom line makes no sense on a full-width row.
- **Forced colours:** the underline is a background, so it names `Highlight`, and it marks the link by itself, so no outline is drawn on it unless it has focus; the other two get the outline `subtle` already had.
- **Checks added:** 6 unit tests (the default adding no class, each look on the current link only, `aria-current` kept, the panel's copy and a group's button carrying it) and 2 real-browser checks: text contrast of 4.5:1 or more in each look, the line's 3:1 against the bar and the solid fill's 3:1 against it, the line's size and place, the solid ring inside the link and visible, and the panel's start-edge bar with the forced-colours behaviour. Shortening the line to 1px was run to see the check fail.
- **Not verified:** contrast was asserted in Purple light and the three looks looked at in Emerald dark, not measured in all four themes.

## Fourth pass: demo links, the default look and the group's menu (2026-10-17)
- **`currentVariant` defaults to `underline`** (was `subtle`), by the maintainer's choice of the house look. Existing checks that meant the tint (link contrast, the stuck-bar outline) now name `currentVariant="subtle"`.
- **Every story's links stay where they are.** A link in a demo bar takes the current mark when chosen, without navigating (`useDemoCurrent` and `preventNavigation` in `NavbarStoryKit.tsx`), and a group that holds the chosen link takes the mark too. The stories' brand is "dbM".
- **Found while testing it: a router's link in a group's menu left the menu open.** `Menu` closes an item through a handler that is skipped when the item's own click called `preventDefault`, which is how a router navigates. The group now closes its menu itself in that case (a unit test with a link that prevents its click, and an interaction story that chooses one).
- **Checks added:** 1 unit test and 2 interaction stories (a chosen link taking the mark, the address unchanged, a disabled link refusing it, and a group taking it).

## Fifth pass: rounded links (2026-10-17)
- **`rounded`** (boolean, default false) rounds each link and group button into a pill, with the focus ring, the tint and the fill following. It is `rounded` and not a variant because that is the one name `Button`, `IconButton` and `Toolbar` use for it (`05` §6); unlike `Toolbar`'s, it does not round the bar, which spans the page. Works in the phone panel too, through the same context, and has no effect on a group's floating menu (that is `Menu`'s look) or on the brand.
- **The underline look has no shape to round**, so `rounded` reads best with `subtle` or `solid`; the Docs say so.
- **Checks added:** 3 unit tests and 1 real-browser check (every link and group button a pill by radius against its own height, a square bar's link not, the bar not a pill, and the keyboard focus ring a pill too). Removing the focus-ring rule was run to see the check fail.

## Sixth pass: the underline grows in (2026-10-17)
- **The underline grows in when a link becomes the current one**, from the link's start edge in the bar (the right edge in right-to-left text, since a transform origin has no logical keyword and `:dir(rtl)` names it) and from the top in the menu panel, in `motion.duration.base` with the `emphasized` easing; always on, with no prop, and none under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No new token.
- **Only on a change.** CSS can't tell a link that is current because it just became so from one drawn current, so a link (and a group's button) remembers whether `current` has turned on since it was drawn, in state compared while rendering, and adds `currentGrow` from that render until it stops being current. A first paint, a re-render and a menu panel opening on a link already current don't animate, and an unrelated update doesn't cut the animation short.
- **A finished animation that fills forwards stays in `getAnimations()`**, so the real-browser check counts only running ones.
- **Checks added:** 5 unit tests (not on a link drawn current, on the one that becomes current only, kept through other updates and dropped when it stops, not in a panel that opens on it, and on a group's button) and 2 real-browser checks (nothing running on load, the line running from the left edge after a click and ending at full size, the old link's line gone, nothing under emulated reduced motion, and the right edge in right-to-left text). Removing the reduced-motion rule was run to see the check fail.
- **Not verified:** how it looks in motion to a person: the checks assert that an animation runs and where it starts, not that its pace feels right.

## Seventh pass: six gaps (2026-10-17)
- **Actions on a phone:** `Navbar.Action` (`hideBelow`) and `Navbar.PanelActions`, which portals its children into the menu panel's footer ([ADR-0093](../adr/0093-navbar-actions-leave-the-bar-by-hidebelow-and-return-in-the-panel-footer-through-a-portal-over-collapsing-every-action-or-writing-the-panel-by-hand.md)).

- **Link extras:** `icon` (a single slot, so the plain name), `badge` (part of the link's name) and `external` (from the `href` like `Link`'s, with a trailing icon and a hidden cue from `labels.externalLink`); none is drawn with `asChild`, as for `Link`.
- **`align` on `Navbar.Links`** (`start`, `center`, `end`), by margins: `center` is the middle of the room between the brand and the actions, not of the bar, which is what a bar with an uneven brand and actions can honestly mean.
- **A sticky `ghost` bar** is transparent until stuck, then a surface and a shadow, with no new prop.
- **`hideOnScroll`**, with a focus exception, and `useHideOnScroll.ts` holding the scroll reading (ADR-0093).
- **Found while testing it:**

  - A finished animation that fills forwards stays in `getAnimations()`.
- **Checks added:** 24 unit tests and 6 real-browser checks (the action leaving the bar and returning at the panel's foot at the width of the links; the skip link clipped, then clear over the corner with contrast and above the bar, reached first by `Tab`; the icon, count and external mark in order on one line at the height of a plain link; the three alignments by position; the ghost bar transparent, then a surface and a shadow, then transparent again; and the bar leaving, returning and staying for focus). Three of them were run against a deliberately broken stylesheet to see them fail.
- **Not verified:** how the hide and the grow feel in motion, a real finger on the panel footer, and the skip link with a screen reader.

## Eighth pass: the final review (2026-10-17)
- **`hideOnScroll` left a strip of the bar on screen when `stickyOffset` was set** (24px of a 59px bar at an offset of 24px). A hidden bar now leaves by its height plus its offset, which the bar hands to the stylesheet as `--navbar-sticky-offset` (from `stickyOffset`, on a sticky bar only; a caller's own `style` still wins). The check runs with an offset, and fails with the offset taken out of the translate.
- **The current item of a group's floating menu had `aria-current` and no mark.** It now takes `text.brand` on `bg.brand-subtle` (a step darker while it is the highlighted item), the tint `NavigationMenu`'s current panel link has, and an outline inside the item in forced colours. It was first a bar at the item's start edge; changed 2026-11-01 at the maintainer's request (a preference change scoped to `Navbar`'s own files, so re-verified: design quality, theming, forced colours, tests).

- **`menuContainer` now holds a group's floating menu as well as the panel,** so a bar given a frame or a shadow root draws all its overlays there (a unit test and a check that the menu is inside the container).
- **JSDoc and the Docs intro** name every part and the main options; `Navbar.Link`'s says what `icon`, `badge` and `external` do.
- **Not verified:** a screen reader, a real finger on the panel, how the hide and the grow feel in motion, and a window resized across the collapse width.
