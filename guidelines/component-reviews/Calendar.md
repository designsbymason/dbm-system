# Calendar — build findings

**Navigation:** Calendar — built 2026-10-05; the first of the two molecules added to the inventory on 2026-10-05 (`04-component-inventory.md`), and the prerequisite of `DatePicker` (organism build order #9). **Finalized 2026-10-05** by the user, after the final review recorded at the end of this file, accepting the limits listed there under "Open" (no screen-reader pass, no real touch device, the animation and the newest grids looked at on a few stories rather than all, the per-render cost of the marker and rule callbacks not profiled with four months on show) and the 12KB bundle budget.

**What it is:** one component, props a union keyed on `mode` (`"single"` default, `"range"`). A `role="group"` named "Calendar" holding a header (previous and next month `Button`s either side of the month heading) and a `role="grid"` `<table>` of seven weekday columns and always six week rows (42 days), each day a real `<button>` in a `gridcell`. `ref`, `className`, `style`, `id`, `data-testid` and the `aria-*` props go on the outer group. The value is a `"YYYY-MM-DD"` string (a `[start, end]` pair in range mode), the month `"YYYY-MM"`; controlled or uncontrolled for both. The decisions behind it — strings not `Date`s, one component with a range mode, a hand-built date model with no dependency — are [ADR-0044](../adr/0044-calendar-dates-are-yyyy-mm-dd-strings-with-a-single-and-a-range-mode-and-a-hand-built-grid.md). The date model is `src/internal/date/dateValue.ts` (54 tests, including a daylight-saving week), shared later with the pickers.

**Decisions put to the user before building:** none beyond the inventory row, which already said single selection first, range for `DateRangePicker`, `min`/`max`/disabled dates, week start, `labels`/`dir`. Two extensions of it were my call and are recorded in the ADR: range mode is built now (so `DateRangePicker` is a thin wrapper), and the month and year picker, two-month layout, week numbers and presets are named and not built.

**Composition checkpoints (06 §9):** compound sub-parts — not applicable (flat props); atom reuse — the month buttons are `Button` (tertiary, slotted onto a `<button>`), their arrows `Icon`, the announcement `useAnnouncement`, the labels `mergeDefined`; the days are plain buttons on purpose (a day is not a `Button`: a grid cell with its own states and a roving tab stop); consumed-atom defects — none; Radix audit — not applicable (hand-built, no Radix calendar exists); cross-part ARIA wiring — the heading's `useId` id names the grid (`aria-labelledby`), the caller's `aria-label` / `aria-labelledby` name the group; composed tab order — previous, next, then the grid's one stop, tested with `Tab` and in a real browser; provides no context.

**Behaviour worth stating:**
- One tab stop on the grid: the date that last had focus, else the chosen date, else today, else the 1st of the month on show. Arrows move a day or a week (left and right swap under `dir="rtl"`), Home and End to the week's ends where `weekStartsOn` puts them, Page Up and Page Down a month (clamped to the month's last day), with Shift a year. A move never goes before `min` or after `max`, and onto another month turns the calendar, then focuses the date once it is drawn.
- Unavailable dates (`min`, `max`, `isDateDisabled`, `disabled`) are `aria-disabled` and stay focusable, so the keys never skip them; only choosing is refused.
- The month buttons are `aria-disabled` (not natively disabled) at `min`'s and `max`'s months and at years 1 and 9999, so focus is never dropped from a button that has just run out.
- Range: the first choice is the start, the second the end, a third starts again, an earlier choice replaces the start; one day long is allowed. While the end is open, the range is previewed to the hovered day (a mouse only) or the focused day (a keyboard).
- Today is read once, after mount, with `useSyncExternalStore` and no server snapshot, so a server render and the first client render agree; the `today` prop overrides it. Until a month is chosen, the month on show follows the chosen date, then `defaultMonth`, then today (UTC month on the server).
- The new month is announced (not the first one) through a status region that is always in the page; `announce={false}` turns it off.

**Defects and surprises found while building:**
- **`IconButton` with `asChild` and `icon` logs a development warning on every render** (found by the real-browser run, which printed it dozens of times while all 22 tests passed). Native `IconButton` can't be `aria-disabled` (its own computed attribute follows the spread), and a natively disabled button that has focus drops it. `Pagination`'s precedent — `Button asChild` onto a `<button>`, with a square class — is what is used.
- **Stepping past December 9999 wrapped to January 9999**: caught by a test; the buttons are now unavailable at both ends of the calendar.
- **The range strip was invisible in light mode.** `bg.brand-subtle` is `#f9f9ff`, 1.04:1 from the page. The strip is `bg.brand-subtle-hover` (1.13–1.14:1 light, 1.40:1 dark), and a hovered plain day is `bg.neutral-subtle-hover` so it still differs from the strip.
- **A neighbouring month's day (`text.tertiary`) fails the 4.5:1 floor on a tinted fill** — measured 4.00–4.15:1 on the hover fill and on the strip in the light themes (and 4.00:1 on the dark neutral hover). It is 4.70:1 on the plain page. On a tinted fill it steps up to `text.secondary`, with the extra selectors keeping an unavailable day dimmed.
- **Two things leaked into the Properties table**: the Playground-only `start` and `end` controls (inferred from the meta `args`, which become argTypes even with none written — they live on the Playground story's `args` and `argTypes`), and `formatNumber`'s function default, which stretched the Default column to 692px in a 644px container (now "—" with the default stated in the description). The table is 644px.
- **Mutation testing found four weak tests**, all fixed: the first-month-not-announced test advanced timers past the point where the announcement had been cleared; the year-1 and year-9999 limits had no test; redundant code the mutants showed dead was removed.

**Contrast, measured in all four themes** (the real token values, in a running Storybook): `text.primary` on `bg.surface` 13.53:1 or more; on `bg.brand-subtle-hover` (the strip) 12.38:1 / 12.46:1 light, 9.66:1 dark; on `bg.neutral-subtle-hover` 12.41:1 light, 6.59:1 dark. `text.on-brand` on `bg.brand` 7.37:1 (Purple) / 6.08:1 (Emerald) light, 7.45:1 / 8.51:1 dark; on the hover fill 10.38:1 or more. `text.secondary` on `bg.surface` (weekday headings) 6.88:1 / 10.47:1. `text.tertiary` (neighbouring days) on `bg.surface` 4.70:1 / 8.21:1; on tinted fills see above. `border.brand` (today's ring) on `bg.surface` 7.37:1 / 6.08:1 light, 7.45:1 / 8.51:1 dark. `border.focus` on `bg.surface` 4.51:1 / 4.16:1 light, 4.99:1 / 6.49:1 dark; on a chosen day the ring is `icon.on-brand` (the same figures as the label). Disabled days are exempt. As elsewhere, none of this is for `bg.canvas` (`text.tertiary` fails there).

**Tokens:** no new tokens and no component tokens: a day's size is `icon-button.size.*` (already matched to `Button`'s height), the rest are existing semantic tokens. The Docs page lists 31 and a test (`Calendar.docs.test.ts`) keeps the list in step with the stylesheet.

**Tests:** 76 unit tests (structure and names, choosing, controlled/uncontrolled, unavailable and read-only/disabled, range, months, keyboard, today, labels and numbers, direction, StrictMode, years 1 and 9999, a server render, jest-axe in single, range, right-to-left, disabled and read-only), 54 for the date model, the Docs-page token guard, and 11 hidden real-browser stories (3 interaction twins; 8 checks: day size against an `IconButton` at all five sizes and seven equal columns; the same grid height in five- and six-week months and the buttons not moving; a narrow container with no overflow; right-to-left geometry and arrow keys; the strip's unbroken run with half-cells at the ends; the focus ring inside the day and its colour on a chosen one; forced colours emulated; the Docs page's own content wrapper). **Mutation check:** 28 deliberate breaks in two rounds; 22 killed at once, 4 survived and were fixed as above, 2 were equivalent (redundant code, removed).

### The key, revised (2026-10-05, at the user's direction)

- **The samples are plain shapes, with no number in them.** The ring, the solid fill and the soft fill are empty shapes; the unavailable look, which in the grid is only a dimmed number, is a short dimmed line where the number would be. `formatNumber` no longer touches the key.
- **The key lists the looks that are on screen, not the ones that could be.** "Selected" appears only once a date is chosen (so clearing a controlled `value` removes it — this was the component's behaviour, not Storybook's; it had listed "Selected" always, by design, until this change), "In range" once a range is started, "Unavailable" when `disabled`, `min`, `max` or `isDateDisabled` is in use, and "Today" once the clock is known (so a server render has no entry for it and nothing to mismatch). The list keeps one row's height, so the first entry appearing moves nothing; a second row appearing or disappearing as entries come and go does move what is below the key, which is the one place this component lets text change its surroundings.
- **Tests:** the key's block was rewritten (entries by choice, by mode and by rule; following a click; the server render; hidden empty samples; labels with `undefined`; jest-axe): 124 unit tests in the Calendar file now. The `formatNumber` test went with the number.

## Short month names in the month field (2026-10-05, at the user's direction)

- **Found by the user:** with `captionLayout="dropdown"` the three longest month names pushed the arrow out of the month field's button. Cause, measured: at `md` the button has about 59px for its text after padding and the arrow, and "September" needs about 75px; the same shortfall at `sm` and `xs`, where the field is narrower still.
- **Fix:** the closed button draws a short name ("Oct"), the list it opens the full one. Each month option holds both forms and CSS chooses where each shows, so `Select` is unchanged: in the list the short one is `display: none`; in the button the full one is clipped to a dot (reduced to `border-width.1`) and kept in the text, so assistive technology reads "October", never "Oct", and the short name is `aria-hidden`. `textValue` is the full name, so typing to find a month still matches it. New label `monthsShort` (twelve names, English "Jan" to "Dec"; a list of the wrong length falls back to English with a development warning).
- **Considered:** truncating with an ellipsis (still loses the name), a wider month field (the buttons and the year field have nothing to spare at `xs`, `sm` and `md`), and short names only at the smaller sizes (inconsistent). Short everywhere is one rule.
- **Tests:** 2 unit tests (both forms in each option and only the full name in the list's roles; `monthsShort` and its fallback) and a real-browser check over every month at every size, 60 buttons: the arrow inside the border box, nothing scrolling sideways, the short name drawn and the full one clipped, and the open list showing full names without overflow. The check was run with the fix removed and fails.
