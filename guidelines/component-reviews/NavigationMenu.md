# NavigationMenu — review findings

**Status:** Built 2026-10-31; not declared Finalized. Open: see "Not verified" below.

## What was built

The site-header navigation with panels: triggers that open panels of grouped links (icons, descriptions, columns) beside plain links. Compound (`NavigationMenu.List`/`Item`/`Trigger`/`Content`/`Link`/`Group`) over Radix `navigation-menu` pinned at 1.2.22 on the shared layer stack, as a disclosure navigation and not `role="menu"` ([ADR-0159](../adr/0159-navigationmenu-wraps-radix-navigation-menu-pinned-at-1-2-22-on-the-shared-layer-stack-as-a-disclosure-navigation-with-one-shared-panel-area-over-a-hand-rolled-menu-role-menu-or-a-panel-under-each-item-only.md)). Drawn `mega` or `stacked`, and inside `Navbar.Links` it follows the bar and is stacked in the menu panel ([ADR-0160](../adr/0160-navigationmenu-is-drawn-stacked-in-the-navbars-menu-panel-and-follows-the-bar-through-a-small-internal-context-over-writing-two-lists-its-own-drawer-or-reading-navbars-private-context.md)).

## Decisions
- Compound parts over a data-driven `items` prop: panels hold arbitrary content, as `Navbar.Group` does.
- One shared, resized panel area by default; `viewport={false}` for a panel under its own item. Two tokens, `navigation-menu.panel-min-width` and `.panel-max-width`; everything else is an existing token. No new colour pairing: the current-page looks are `Navbar`'s and the panel is `Menu`'s surface, border and shadow.
- `NavigationMenu.Link` draws its own `<a>` (and `Slot` for `asChild`) rather than composing `Link` or sharing `Navbar.Link`'s, as `Navbar` and `Sidebar` do. Three components now draw one with the same disabled and external handling; extract a shared guard to `internal/` if a fourth needs it.
- No `controllerRef`: a controlled `value` opens and closes it from outside.
- No indicator arrow in v1 (additive later; it needs a right-to-left offset check).
- Bundle: 6.9KB of JS and 3.7KB of CSS after the second round (4.1KB and 2.8KB at first), inside the default budgets, so no override. `Navbar` went from 20.0KB to 20.05KB of JS against its 22KB.

## What was found while building it
- **The primitive's focus stop fails an accessibility run while a panel is open.** It keeps an `aria-hidden`, `tabindex="0"` span beside an open trigger to hand `Tab` into the panel, and axe reports `aria-hidden-focus` (the real-browser run caught it on a story that started open; jsdom did not). It is the primitive's own and goes when the panel closes, so no story starts open, every check closes its panel and waits for it to be gone, and the Docs page says so. A consumer who runs an accessibility check with a panel open will see it.
- **A synthetic click re-opens a panel closed within 200ms.** `userEvent.click` moves the pointer over the trigger first, which starts the primitive's hover timer; closing sooner than `delayDuration` is undone by it when it fires. A person can't hover and press `Escape` that fast, so the checks wait 300ms after a press.
- **The slide side is physical.** `data-motion="from-start"` is the left in both directions (a later trigger in right-to-left text, which is on the left, gives `from-start`), so the first version's mirroring custom property double-flipped it. The keyframes now move by a fixed side, checked in a real browser in right-to-left.
- **`Tab` after `Escape` can land in a panel still easing out.** The panel stays in the page for its exit animation (about 150ms), so the checks wait for it to be gone before tabbing on.
- **A key and a click switch panels differently under a synthetic pointer.** The check that moves between panels uses a key; the same step by click opened the second panel and then a hover timer raced it.
- **The viewport's size is applied from a `ResizeObserver`,** which a hidden or throttled page delays; the checks wait for it rather than for a fixed time. In use it resizes within a frame.
- **`onSelect` is the primitive's own event, not a React one,** so `NavigationMenuLinkProps` omits the native `onSelect` and types it as `(event: Event) => void`.

## After the first build
- **The shared panel area shrank to the menu.** It is a flex item of a frame as wide as the menu, so in a bar (where the menu is as wide as its links) it took the frame's width and clipped its own panel, and in a narrow window it narrowed with the nav while its links kept their width. It now keeps the width the primitive measured (`flex: none`, the border added to the measure and not taken from it), and the panel's width is capped by the room from where it hangs to the screen's edge (`--navigation-menu-room`, written from an effect, minus its borders), so its links wrap instead of being cut off. Below 40rem a group's links run in one column.
- **A caller's `onClick` that prevents the default stopped the panel closing.** The primitive composes a handler given to its link, and a composed handler that calls `preventDefault()` (a router's does) skips the primitive's own closing. The caller's handler is now on the element inside the primitive's link, merged by the slot, so a panel closes whatever the click's default. Demo links prevent navigation, so a story keeps its page.

## Second round: what a larger header needs (2026-11-01)
Decisions in [ADR-0161](../adr/0161-navigationmenu-keeps-its-panels-on-the-screen-by-a-measured-room-and-takes-a-split-trigger-a-path-for-the-current-page-and-item-held-disclosure-state-over-a-fixed-width-a-trigger-that-is-one-or-the-other-or-a-route-hook.md). Added: panels kept on the screen by a measured room (width and height, so links wrap and a tall panel scrolls), `panelAlign` `end` and `auto`, `panelSize`, `orientation="vertical"`, a split trigger (`href`), `badge`, `onIntent`, a per-trigger `delayDuration`, `currentPath` and `currentMatch` with a trigger that follows its panel, the stacked form's `exclusive`, `defaultOpen` and current-open start with an animated height, `Feature`, `Footer`, `layout="columns"`, a linked group heading, `descriptionLines`, an indicator, `onOpenChangeComplete`, a `controllerRef`, and `internal/linkGuard.ts` shared with `Navbar` and `Sidebar`.
- **Found while building it:** `useRoom` writes the room from an effect and settles `auto` into state; the stacked form's open state moved from the root to each item so `exclusive={false}` and a current item that starts open work; the primitive's indicator only draws once it has measured a trigger, which a test DOM never does, so it is checked in a real browser; `full` spans the nearest *positioned* ancestor, which a non-sticky `Navbar` header is not, so the checks use a sticky one and the Docs say so.
- **Still open:** the primitive's hidden focus stop still fails an accessibility scan while a panel is open (its own, not patched); a real finger, Safari, Firefox and a screen reader are still not verified; a link wrapped in a component of the caller's is not seen when finding the current page.

## Third round: layout and the demos (2026-11-01)
- **A panel's room ends at the nearest clipping ancestor,** not only the window: the width and height it is capped by are measured against the window narrowed by every ancestor that scrolls or hides its overflow, and re-measured when one resizes, so a panel in a narrower box (a Docs canvas, a scrolling main) wraps its groups and keeps its footer and featured block whole.
- **A split trigger is one surface:** the wrapper carries the hover, open and current-page fill and the caret stretches to the wrapper's height, so a badge that makes the label taller no longer leaves the two halves tinted at different heights.
- **In a vertical menu the current page is a bar on the start edge** (the left in left-to-right text, the right in right-to-left), as in the stacked form, not a line under the text.
- **A split trigger's own address counts toward the current page,** so the trigger is current when its `href` is the page.
- **Demos:** choosing a link makes it the current page (and its section's trigger), going nowhere; every story spreads its live controls and switches off only the ones it fixes or never reads; the Controls panel order is the Properties table's order, held by a docs test; `defaultValue` shows "None" for nothing open; the bar story's landmark takes the Playground's label.
- **Checked by driving every control in the running preview** (an arg update through the channel, comparing the canvas): the ones that did not change the closed canvas are behaviour a closed menu does not show (timers, a panel's size while closed, `exclusive` until something is pressed, `currentMatch` until a path needs it), and each was checked open or in a real-browser check; the `indicator` needs a measured trigger, which the preview pane's throttled layout never gave, so it is checked by the real-browser check that finds its arrow over the open trigger.

## Fourth round: rows and lines (2026-11-01)
- **A link in a panel is the whole width of its column,** so a hover or the current page tints the row; the description (not the link) is what is kept to a line and a half of the least panel width, so a long sentence still doesn't make the panel as wide as itself.
- **A split trigger's current-page line is the wrapper's:** one line under the label and the caret together (a bar on the start edge in a column and in the stacked form), and the link draws none of its own, so the line belongs to the same surface as the hover and the fill.
- Docs page: the related cards' previews are different real components (a bar, a button with a caret, a column of rows, a tab list).

## Checks
- Unit tests, 57 for the component itself (roles, the landmark's name, opening by press and key, arrow keys, names and cues, controlled and uncontrolled `value`, disabled parts, `onSelect`, same-named props not overriding computed attributes on every part, refs and ids, current looks, the stacked form, inside a `Navbar`, jest-axe closed, open, stacked and right to left) and the newer parts (current page from a path, the stacked form's state, split trigger, badge, intent and delay, group heading, feature and footer, controller and completion, vertical, own-panel alignment, a split caret's name, the development warnings), 31 server-render and hydration tests (the plain tree and one with every newer part), and a docs guard of 56 (tokens, Properties tables, Controls order).
- 33 real-browser checks: the panel and pointer (hover open and the gap, a link the top element, the shared area resizing, keyboard in and out, 24px targets at every size and in every panel part, a panel under a sticky bar, a narrow window, a clipping box, a tall panel, `auto` and `end`, the sizes, vertical, the indicator, a split trigger's hover, surface and line, a link filling its column, a chosen demo link becoming the current page), forced colours and reduced motion, right to left (the panel, the indicator, `end` and a split trigger's order, a vertical panel's side, the arrow keys, the start-edge bar), a badge on the solid look, and focus kept across a swap of forms.

## Final review round (2026-11-01)
- **A group heading link was 16px tall** (below the 24px floor): it has the floor now, and the 24px check covers every target in an open panel at the smallest and largest size.
- **A badge vanished on the solid current-page look** (a solid brand pill on a brand fill): it is drawn subtle there, as `Navbar`'s is.
- **The indicator was at the wrong end in right-to-left text:** its stylesheet set a logical inset beside the physical `left` the primitive writes, which in a right-to-left list resolved to the far edge; the inset is gone.
- **Focus dropped when the menu swapped between the mega and the stacked form** (a width map crossing its step): reproduced with a real check, then fixed: the focused stop is remembered by its place among the stops and the same one is focused in the new form, only when focus was lost with the old one.
- **Development warnings** (once per message) for what is ignored: `indicator` without the shared area or in a column or stacked, `exclusive` in the mega form, `defaultValue` with a controlled `value`, `defaultOpen` outside an uncontrolled stacked menu, `forceMount` and the dismissal callbacks in a stacked panel, `panelSize="full"` with no positioned ancestor, and a link or trigger with no name.
- **A split trigger's `aria-label` names its caret** in place of `labels.togglePanel` (and its `ref` and other native props go to the caret, which the JSDoc now says).
- **Every "Show code" snippet was typechecked against the real components** (planting a bad prop first to see it caught), the Playground's is built from the live controls and includes `defaultValue`, and two snippets that marked a current link and gave a `currentPath` at once were made to say one thing.

## Not verified
- Safari, Firefox, a real touch screen (a tap opens by click, which the unit test and the primitive's pointer-type handling cover, but not a real finger), a screen reader, a phone with an on-screen keyboard.
- The Docs page was looked at in Purple light and Emerald dark only. The text, ring, fill and line pairings the menu uses were measured in all four themes (every text pairing is at least 4.5:1, the lowest 4.63:1; the focus ring at least 4.16:1; the underline and brand fills at least 6:1).
- `Shift+Tab` from the first link of a panel back to its trigger was done with real keys in the preview and not in a check: the primitive hands focus through its hidden stop, which a synthetic key doesn't reproduce.
- A panel is capped by the room to the edge of what can show it and its links wrap, so it is not moved to fit; a very narrow room (under the least panel width) still takes that width.
- The primitive's hidden focus stop still fails an accessibility scan while a panel is open; it is the primitive's own and not patched.
