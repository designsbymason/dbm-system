# TableOfContents — build findings

**Navigation:** TableOfContents — built 2026-10-04, item 33 (the last) in the molecule build order (`04-component-inventory.md`). **Finalized 2026-10-04** by the user, after the final review recorded at the end of this file, accepting the limits listed there under "Not done, and why" (no screen-reader pass, real-device or iOS Safari check; the hash correction untested in a real browser; the dropdown panel's dark and Emerald look checked by contrast not by eye).

**What it is:** an outline of one page. A flat-props component (not compound): a `<nav>` named by its visible heading (or `labels.navigation`, or the caller's `aria-label`/`aria-labelledby`) around a `ul` of `Link` atoms going to `#id`. `ref`, `id`, `data-testid` and the `aria-*` props go on the `<nav>`, as does `className`/`style` (there is no frame, so no split). Entries are an `items` list (`id`, `label`, `level` 1–4) or, with no `items`, read in the browser from `contentRef` (default: the document) by `selector` (default `h2, h3`), indented by how far each heading is below the highest found, re-read when the content changes. It renders nothing with no entries.

**Decisions put to the user before building** (the inventory row had no notes): where entries come from (both `items` and reading the page) and whether to follow the scroll (yes, with a controlled override). Not an ADR: no fork the numbered docs don't already cover.

**One difference from what was proposed:** the current entry is worked out from a scroll listener (rAF-throttled) that finds the last heading to have reached the top, not from an `IntersectionObserver`, since "the last heading that has passed the top" is a position question an observer only answers indirectly. Before any heading has reached the top, the first one showing in the area is marked (found by the user on the first Playground: the first heading sits a little below the top edge, so nothing was marked on load); a first heading still below the fold marks nothing. The end of the page marks the last entry, as a short last section could never reach the top.

**Composition checkpoints (06 §9):** compound sub-parts — not applicable; atom reuse — every entry is the `Link` atom (`underline="none"`, its focus ring, its `:visited` rules overridden element-qualified); consumed-atom defects — none found; Radix audit — not applicable; cross-part ARIA wiring — the heading's `id` (from `useId`) names the `<nav>` through `aria-labelledby`, tested with a caller's `aria-label` and `aria-labelledby` taking over; composed tab order — one stop per entry, nothing else; no context provided.

**Behaviour worth stating:**
- A click scrolls the container or the page so the section sits `scrollOffset` pixels from the top, sets the address to `#id` with `replaceState` (no history entry per click), and marks the entry. A modifier or non-primary click, a click already cancelled, and a target that isn't there are left to the browser.
- A key press (`click` with `detail` 0) moves focus to the section, giving it `tabindex="-1"` unless it has one; a mouse click doesn't, as a plain anchor wouldn't.
- The clicked entry is held while the smooth scroll passes the headings in between, and the outline catches up once scroll events stop for 150ms.
- `defaultActiveId` stands until the first scroll; `activeId` makes scrolling inert and leaves the decision to the prop (a click only asks, per `05` §3's controlled-part rule).
- The motion preference is read when a click needs it, never at render; the marker never changes the text's weight, so nothing reflows (measured in a real browser).

**Defects and surprises:** none found in the build beyond lint (a useless assignment, a non-interactive `tabindex` on the demo's scroll box and on a test heading, each fixed or disabled with a stated reason). One test (`motion read at render`) was dropped: `Link` and `Icon` read `matchMedia` themselves, so it measured the wrong thing.

**Contrast and shape (from the recorded token measurements, no new pairing measured):** on `bg.surface`, entries `text.secondary` 6.88:1 / 10.47:1; hover and the neutral current entry `text.primary` 14.11:1 / 13.53:1; the brand current entry `text.brand` 7.37:1 (Purple) / 6.08:1 (Emerald) light, 7.45:1 / 6.49:1 dark; the bar `border.brand` 7.37:1 / 6.08:1 light, 7.45:1 / 8.51:1 dark; the neutral bar `border.neutral-strong` 4.70:1 / 6.08:1. A brand outline is not for `bg.canvas` (dark 3.63:1 Purple, 3.16:1 Emerald); the neutral tone is. The marker is a thicker, coloured bar plus `aria-current`, never colour alone; in forced colours the track is `CanvasText` and the marker `Highlight` (borders, not backgrounds). Every entry is at least 24px tall (`space.6`) at every size.

**Tokens:** no new tokens and no component tokens; the indent per level is `space.2`/`3`/`4` by size.

**Tests:** 35 unit tests (structure and names, roles, empty list, labels with `undefined`, ref and pass-through, levels and clamping, jest-axe; reading the page — scope, selector, `data-toc-level`, a heading with no id, an invalid selector, later and removed headings, not thrown off by its own update; the current entry — default, following the scroll, starting where the page is, `defaultActiveId` standing, `scrollOffset`, a scrolling container, the end of the page, controlled, StrictMode; following a link — page and container scroll, smooth and reduced, offset, modified/middle/cancelled/missing-target clicks, focus after a key press only, an existing `tabindex` kept, the hold until scrolling stops; listeners removed), a Docs-page token guard (`TableOfContents.docs.test.ts`), and 5 hidden real-browser stories (follows the scroll and a click through a real scrolling box, including the address and the last section at the end; a key press moves focus; every size's entries are 24px tall; the current entry changes no entry's size; right-to-left puts the marker on the right). **Mutation check:** five deliberate breaks (offset ignored, always focusing, no end-of-page rule, no hold, the non-primary-click guard removed) — one survived (the button guard) and a test was added that kills it.

**Self-verification (real runs, 2026-10-04):** `pnpm lint`, `pnpm build`, `pnpm test` (6,089 unit tests) and the full Chromium Storybook project (143 files, 1,168 tests) pass; `check-component-bundle-size` reports 2.98KB JS / 1.01KB CSS. The Docs page opened in a running Storybook with no console errors; the Playground scrolled to a section and showed the right entry marked, looked at in light (Purple), in dark (Emerald, both tones), and at a 375px viewport.

**Not yet done (for the final review):** a keyboard walk through the Docs page's own Playground; all four themes by eye (three looked at); a real touch device; a screen-reader pass (checked by structure and names, not by listening); forced colours looked at (the CSS is written and the structure tested, not emulated); a server render (the reading path guards `document` inside an effect, but no SSR test renders it); the story-controls driving check from `07` §5.

**Known limits:** reading headings costs a first paint with no outline on the server; a long outline doesn't scroll its own current entry into view (put it in a box with a height); `scrollOffset` is a number of pixels, not a token step (it matches a header's real height); a heading with no `id` is skipped, not given one; the outline isn't sticky on its own (wrap it in `Affix`).

## Follow-ups after the user's first look (2026-10-04)

- **A first heading showing on load wasn't marked** (component bug): the first heading sits a little below the top edge, so none had "reached the top". Before any has, the first one showing in the scrolling area is now marked; one below the fold marks nothing.
- **Static demo stories navigated Storybook on a click** (story bug): their entries point at ids that aren't on the page, so the browser followed `#id`. A `Static` wrapper cancels the click in those stories.
- **A page scroll cleared the marked entry of an outline with no sections on the page** (component bug, seen as most Variants stories showing no current entry on the Docs page): the scroll listener measured against nothing and set the mark to undefined. With none of the entries' targets on the page the mark is now left alone (a unit test, killed by removing the guard).
- **The `items` fields table was a plain Markdown table**, unlike the main Properties table: it is now a `PropertiesTable` from a hidden `TableOfContentsItem.stories.tsx` (ADR-0013's pattern; the hidden file needs one story to be indexed).
- **Snippets re-checked**: every snippet typechecked as written against the real components (one attribute to a line); the sticky-offset snippet now matches its story (a scrolling box with a 40px header, `scrollOffset={40}`).

## `highlightActive` (2026-10-04, at explicit direction)

An optional boolean (**default true since the same day, at explicit direction**; `false` for the marker bar alone) that fills the current entry's link with its tone's subtle background: `bg.brand-subtle` for `brand`, `bg.neutral-subtle` for `neutral`. The marker bar and `aria-current` stay, so the fill is never the only cue; in forced colours the fill is dropped by the browser and the bar and `Highlight` text remain. New pairings, measured: `text.brand` on `bg.brand-subtle` 7.03:1 (Purple) / 5.83:1 (Emerald) light, 8.29:1 / 7.22:1 dark; `text.primary` on `bg.neutral-subtle` 13.53:1 / 9.66:1; the bars on those fills stay above 4.3:1. Seen in light and dark: the neutral fill reads clearly in both; in dark the brand fill (`gray.950`, a step *darker* than the surface and about 1.1:1 from it) is very faint, an accepted consequence of the token's value rather than a fault of this prop. Two new tokens in the Docs table; one unit test and one real-browser check (the fill on the current entry only, in each tone, equal to the token's resolved colour).

## Feature round (2026-10-04, at explicit direction)

Six gaps named in a feature-completeness look, all built: (1) the current entry is kept in view in an outline that scrolls on its own — the nearest overflowing ancestor other than the page and `scrollContainerRef` is moved with `scrollTop` (never `scrollIntoView`, which would scroll the page too); (2) `collapse` (`never`/`auto`/`always`) with `open`/`defaultOpen`/`onOpenChange`; (3) `sticky` and `stickyOffset` on `Affix`, as `Alert` does; (4) `scrollToHash`; (5) `minLevel`/`maxLevel`; (6) per-entry `icon`, `trailing` and `disabled`.

**Decisions inside the fold:** the button is the `Button` atom (secondary, full width, a caret) named by `labels.title`, and the visible title is hidden while folded. `auto` is a media query at 639.98px in CSS and the same question through `matchMedia` in script (for what choosing an entry should do). **Keyboard focus is never dropped by the fold** (06 §9): choosing an entry with a key press moves focus to the section, with the mouse to the button, `Escape` from inside returns it to the button, and a list that holds focus stays open however the screen narrows (a real-browser story closes the state from outside with focus on a link). Closing is a click handler's job, so no effect fights a controlled `open`.

**Behaviour notes:** `scrollToHash` runs after the browser's own jump and again when the headings it names arrive later; `maxLevel` below `minLevel` is raised to it; a `disabled` entry is `aria-disabled` and ignored by clicks (`Link`'s own guard), but still scrollspy-marked as it is still a section; `sticky` composes `Affix asChild`, so `className`/`style`/`ref` stay on the nav and no frame is added.

**Tests:** 91 unit tests now (levels, extras, sticky, hash, scroll-in-box, folding), 8 hidden real-browser stories added or extended (a sticky outline stays at the top of its box; the current entry scrolls into view in a long outline without moving the page; fold open and `Escape`; `auto` on a phone viewport; focus holds the list open). **Mutation check:** thirteen breaks across both rounds; every one killed after one weak test (the `scrollToHash={false}` one, which rerendered with the default) was rewritten.

**Not done:** a real-browser check of the hash correction (a story can't reload with an address, so only unit tests and the logic cover it); `collapse="auto"` was checked by a phone-viewport story, not on a device; the focus hand-off after a mouse click is unit-tested (jsdom focus), not tried with a screen reader.

## Numbered, moving marker, collapsible groups (2026-10-04, at explicit direction)

Three more from the lower-priority list.
- **`numbered` (+ `formatNumber`):** hierarchical numbers from the drawn levels (a skipped level counts as one; `minLevel`/`maxLevel` renumber from what is left); the number is plain text inside the link, so it is in the accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3). New component token `table-of-contents.number-min-width` (`2.5ch`).
- **`movingMarker`:** one `aria-hidden` bar in a `.body` wrapper around the list, placed over the marked row from its measured box (in a layout effect each render, plus a `ResizeObserver`), sliding with `motion.duration.moderate`; it starts placed rather than travelling (animation enabled a frame after the first placement), is hidden when the row has no box (a folded list), and is a *border* so forced colours keeps it (`Highlight`). Under reduced motion it doesn't slide. The per-entry coloured bar is switched off while it is on.
- **`collapsibleGroups` + `groupsDefaultOpen`:** a group is an entry with deeper entries after it; its `IconButton` has `aria-expanded` and the name `labels.groupToggle(label)`. A group's openness is: the person's choice if made, else the default (`groupsDefaultOpen`, or "contains the current entry" when false); **a group holding keyboard focus is always open** (06 §9). A closed group holding the current entry carries the marker on its heading (`data-toc-marked`) and doesn't claim `aria-current`. Hidden entries are not rendered.

**Tests:** 96 unit tests for the component, plus 2 more hidden real-browser stories (the marker's box equals the marked row's box before and after a scroll, and no row carries its own bar; a group folds, opens, follows the page, and shows the marker on a folded heading). **Mutation check:** nine breaks across these features; two survived (a pointless cleanup line, removed; a hidden-row guard, now tested).

**Mobile recommendation (given, not a feature):** `collapse="auto"`, not sticky, `maxLevel` 2 where the outline is long; no `movingMarker` (nobody watches it on a phone), no `collapsibleGroups` inside an already-folded list.

**Not done:** the moving marker with a wrapping entry in a real narrow column was measured by the geometry check but not looked at on a device; the group buttons add a tab stop each, which the docs say to weigh for short outlines.

## `data-testid` on an `items` entry (2026-10-04, at explicit direction)

An entry can carry `data-testid`, set on its link (the element a test clicks and reads `aria-current` from). `className`, `style` and `id` were considered and left out on purpose: entries get their look from `size`, `tone`, `highlightActive` and the level indent (`trailing` and `icon` cover a one-off emphasis), and an entry's `id` already means the section it points at. One unit test (it lands on the link only, and nowhere else); a deliberate break was caught.

## The dropdown for a phone: `foldedStyle="dropdown"` and `sticky="folded"` (2026-10-04, at explicit direction)

**Decisions put to the user and taken as recommended:** the overlay is the existing `Popover` (focus return, outside press, `Escape` and edge collision are already reviewed; in-place expansion would have been hand-written); it extends `collapse` with a style prop rather than a separate `compact` prop; `sticky` also accepts `"folded"`.

**A disclosure of real links, not a `Select`:** a `Select` announces a form control and its options aren't links. The trigger is the `Button` atom (secondary, full width) whose text is the current entry on one line with an ellipsis, so the bar never resizes as it changes; its accessible name says the outline's name first ("On this page: Setup", so the visible text is in the name, WCAG 2.5.3) and it is **not a live region**. The panel is a named dialog holding a named `nav` and the same links; it opens with focus on the current entry and scrolls it into view; the list is drawn once at a time (the inline copy isn't rendered while the dropdown shows, so an entry's `data-testid` stays unique). `onCloseAutoFocus` takes over the focus return: see the defect below.

**Folded-ness in script:** `collapse="auto"` is now `useSyncExternalStore` over the same media query the stylesheet uses (server snapshot false), replacing the one-off `matchMedia` question; the panel can only be open while the outline is folded, whatever the open state says at a wide width.

**`sticky="folded"`:** pure CSS (`position: sticky` on the nav below the breakpoint, or always for `collapse="always"`, on `bg.surface` with a `border.default` rule and `z-index.sticky`, with `stickyOffset` as a custom property), not `Affix`, so it needs no script to know when to apply. **The headings are kept clear of the bar automatically:** while the nav is folded and computed `position: sticky`, its height plus its `top` is added to the offset a click, the `#id` correction and the scroll-spy measure against. `sticky` true still means the `Affix` box.

**Tests:** 102 unit tests in this file now (name and current entry, not a live region, the panel and its landmarks, one list at a time, one `data-testid`, choosing closes and scrolls, focus after a key press and `Escape`, `auto` wide and narrow, controlled `open`, jest-axe closed and open, the sticky class and custom property, the offset test with the bar's height, `sticky` true still Affix) and 3 hidden real-browser stories (the bar stays at the top of a scrolling box, its button and width never change as it names the section being read, the panel opens and a choice leaves the section below the bar; the keyboard path; `collapse="auto"` on a phone viewport is a sticky bar with no inline links). **Mutation check:** seven breaks; the one that survived was dead code and was removed.

**Looked at:** the phone story at 375px in light, panel open with the current entry marked. **Not done:** dark and Emerald by eye for the panel; a real device (iOS Safari's moving toolbar against `position: sticky`); a screen-reader pass of the dialog and the focus return; right-to-left for the dropdown (the panel is as wide as the button and aligned to its start edge, which follows the direction, not exercised in a browser).

**Defect found by the user, fixed (2026-10-04): choosing an entry in the dropdown didn't go to its section** (seen on the Docs page, where the page itself also scrolls). Closing the panel made Radix hand focus back to the button with a plain `focus()`, which scrolls to reveal the focused element and cut the in-progress smooth scroll to the section short, so the entry was marked and the panel closed but the box barely moved. It never showed in the unit tests (jsdom has no scrolling) or the first browser tests (a page that didn't scroll). A hidden story now puts the demo in a scrolling page and waits for the section to arrive below the bar (it failed before the fix); `onCloseAutoFocus` now prevents Radix's focus return and gives focus back with `focus({ preventScroll: true })`, only when nothing else holds focus (a key press has already put it on the section). Checked with real mouse clicks on the Docs page.

## Group buttons are tertiary (2026-10-04, at explicit direction)

The `collapsibleGroups` buttons are a `tertiary` `IconButton` (were `ghost`), and the stylesheet no longer overrides their colour: they take `IconButton`'s own tertiary colours (brand-tinted: `text.brand` family, as verified for that variant), so the `icon.default` token row left the Docs page's table.

## Final review (2026-10-04)

A full `06-engineering-standards.md` §9 pass against the code as it stands, with real runs.

**Fixed:**
1. **The dropdown's accessible name had a hard-coded colon** ("On this page:" in a visually hidden span): punctuation the component wrote itself and a consumer couldn't translate (ADR-0021). The name is now `labels.dropdownTrigger(title, current)`, an `aria-label` used when the entry's label is plain text and built from the same text the button shows (the number too, so WCAG 2.5.3 holds with `numbered`); a label that isn't text keeps a content-derived name. Two tests, and a break (spreading `labels` instead of `mergeDefined`) killed them.
2. **The Properties table's `labels` row was stale:** it named two of the four parts (the `groupToggle` added with the groups, and the new `dropdownTrigger`, were missing), and the type's `@default` showed an object missing both. `formatNumber`'s default (`String`) was undocumented and is now shown.
3. **The Docs page didn't say where the dropdown's and the groups' names are translated**; one sentence now lists every part of `labels` and `formatNumber`.

**Checked and passing, with what was run:** hard-coded values (the one literal is the 640px media condition `var()` can't hold, documented); `{...props}` before every computed attribute; every Properties row has a description and the right default (read from the rendered page against the type's `@default` tags); every Playground control drives the canvas (each changed through the channel and the markup compared, including `foldedStyle` and `groupsDefaultOpen`, which depend on another control); **contrast measured from computed styles in all four themes** (active text on its fill 5.83:1 or more, rest text 6.88:1 or more, bars 4.34:1 or more on the surface and on the fill — the dark brand fill is 1.11:1 from the surface, the accepted trade-off recorded for `highlightActive`); atom reuse (`Link`, `Button`, `IconButton`, `Icon`, `Affix`, `Popover`, `VisuallyHidden`); the full tab order across open groups, the dropdown and the inline fold (a real-browser story, broken once to prove it bites); fold, dropdown and group buttons at least 24px at every size; right-to-left for the marker, the group buttons and the dropdown; forced colours for the track, the marker and the current entry (emulated, broken once); server rendering of every option, hydration of the server's markup on a narrow screen with no warning; a render of 3,000 entries with groups in 214ms (jsdom), so no memoising was added; every one of the 23 "Show code" snippets typechecked as written; bundle 8.02KB JS / 2.80KB CSS; `pnpm audit` unchanged (the one ignored `braces` advisory).

**Tests at this point:** 107 unit tests in `TableOfContents.test.tsx` (plus the Docs-page token guard), 25 hidden real-browser stories, lint, typecheck, build, the full unit suite (6,180) and the full Chromium Storybook suite (1,197) green.

**Not done, and why:** a screen-reader pass (checked by structure and accessible names only); a real touch device and iOS Safari's moving toolbar against `position: sticky`; the hash correction in a real browser (a story can't reload with an address); the dropdown panel in dark and Emerald by eye (contrast was measured, not looked at); `onKeyDown`/`onFocus` a consumer puts on the root also hear events from the dropdown's panel, because React bubbles portal events to the React parent (a consumer's own handler only; the component's own handlers ignore them).

**Additive feature (2026-11-08, [ADR-0197](../adr/0197-tableofcontents-gets-a-layout-row-of-pills-with-a-dropdown-for-each-ones-children-over-a-second-outline-component-or-a-page-drawn-row.md)), approved by the maintainer:** `layout="row"` (pills, a popover for the children of a pill, sticky under a header, sideways scroll) and a CSS-length `stickyOffset` for `"folded"` and the row. The list layout is unchanged; the review above stands.

**Row redrawn on `NavigationMenu` (2026-11-08, [ADR-0198](../adr/0198-the-tableofcontents-row-is-drawn-by-navigationmenu-and-layout-takes-a-width-map-over-keeping-the-hand-built-pills-and-a-two-element-pattern-only.md)), approved by the maintainer:** the row's pills and panels are `NavigationMenu`'s (a molecule importing an organism, as `Toolbar` does with `Menu`), with `currentType="location"`, faded ends for a row longer than the room, and a width map for `layout`.
