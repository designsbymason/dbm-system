# TagsInput — build and review findings

**Inputs & Forms:** TagsInput — built 2026-10-10, item 22 in the ⚪ build order and the last molecule (`04-component-inventory.md`). **Finalized 2026-10-10** by the user, after the final review recorded at the end of this file. Decision: [ADR-0049](../adr/0049-tagsinput-draws-chips-and-its-own-entry-in-one-wrapping-box-and-adds-on-enter-separator-blur-or-paste-over-the-input-atom-or-a-combobox.md).

**What it is:** a field holding a list of short typed values as removable chips. Flat props, `ref`, `id`, `data-testid` and `aria-*` on the entry `<input>`; `className`/`style` on the outer box. `value`/`defaultValue`/`onValueChange` (a `string[]`, a new array each time), `inputValue`/`defaultInputValue`/`onInputValueChange` (the pending text), `placeholder`, `size`, `tone`/`variant` (Tag's own; `brand` and `subtle` by default), `separators`, `addOnBlur`, `allowDuplicates`, `maxTags`, `maxTagLength`, `transform`, `validate`, `clearable`, `required`, `hasError`/`disabled`/`readOnly`, `name`/`form` (one hidden input per tag), `autoComplete`/`inputMode`/`enterKeyHint`/`spellCheck`/`autoCapitalize`, `formatNumber`, `labels`.

**Composition checkpoints (06 §9):** compound sub-parts — not applicable (flat); atom reuse — `Tag` (removable, one step below the field), `Icon`, `FieldError`, `VisuallyHidden`, `useAnnouncement`; the entry is its own `<input>`, not `Input`, for the reason in the ADR; consumed-atom defects — none found; Radix audit — not applicable; cross-part ARIA wiring — a `FormField`'s `id`/`aria-labelledby`/`aria-describedby` land on the entry (and the group), tested; composed tab order — one stop at the entry (the chips are reached with the arrow keys, [ADR-0050](../adr/0050-tagsinput-chips-are-one-tab-stop-with-arrow-key-movement-and-tag-lets-its-remove-button-leave-the-tab-order.md)), then clear-all, forwards and back (tested in jsdom and Chromium); no context provided.

**Second feature round (2026-10-10, from the gap list, at explicit direction):**
- **`invalidBehavior: "refuse" | "flag"` (default `refuse`):** with `flag`, a tag `validate` rejects is added anyway, drawn in `Tag`'s `danger` tone with its reason read after it (hidden text), the entry marked `aria-invalid`, and a surrounding form refused through the hidden validating control (`labels.invalid`). A repeat and a tag past `maxTags` are still refused. Flagging is derived from `validate` each render, so a tag that arrives through `value` is flagged too, and a corrected or removed one is not.
- **`onTagAdd(tag, { source })` and `onTagRemove(tag, { index, source })`:** per tag, after `onValueChange`; sources `enter`/`separator`/`blur`/`paste` and `backspace`/`button`/`keyboard`/`clear`.
- **`showCount`:** `count/max` after the tags, only with `maxTags`, through `formatNumber`.
- **Roving chip model ([ADR-0050](../adr/0050-tagsinput-chips-are-one-tab-stop-with-arrow-key-movement-and-tag-lets-its-remove-button-leave-the-tab-order.md)):** the chips leave the tab order; the arrow key toward them from the start of the entry, then arrows, Home, End, Delete/Backspace and Escape. Keys flip in right-to-left (checked in Chromium). Needed one additive `Tag` prop, `removeTabStop` (default `true`), recorded in [Tag.md](Tag.md).
- **`maxVisible`:** collapses to that many chips and a `+N more` `Tag` button in the chips' own tone and variant (`aria-expanded`) while neither the entry nor a chip has focus and it has not been opened; "Show less" returns. Hidden tags are still submitted. It collapses on focus leaving, so what is typed or removed is always in view.
- **A collapsed row stays one line (found by the user in Storybook):** with `maxVisible`, a first row filled by the chips and the `+N more` button left less room than the entry's minimum width, so the empty typing area wrapped onto a row of its own. While collapsed the entry now gives up its minimum width and stays on the chips' row; it has the minimum back once the field is used and the row opens. A hidden check asserts one line and fails without the rule.
- **Chips sit evenly in the box (found by the user in Storybook):** a chip was about 17px from the side of the box and 5px from its top. Two causes: the box took `Input`'s side padding, which is sized for text, and the invisible height-holding chip took a further 4px (the box's gap followed it, and its margin cancelled only half). The sizer now cancels the whole gap, and the padding is the same on all four sides; text (the placeholder, what is typed, the counter) is given `Input`'s inset less that padding, so an empty field's text still starts exactly where an `Input`'s does. Heights are unchanged. A hidden check measures both at five sizes and fails without the change.
- **Storybook controls and snippets audited (2026-10-10, at the user's direction):** `maxVisible` and `maxTags` had no value in most stories, so their number controls were inert "Set number" placeholders; both are off at the meta level and given a number control and a value only where they are the point (`Playground`, `Limit`, `With a counter`, `Collapsing a long row`), and `showCount` is hidden until `maxTags` is set. Every control of every story was checked live for an undefined arg (none left). The types file, the stories' `argTypes`, the Docs page's `propOrder` and the rendered Controls panel and Properties table now share one order (read live after a dev-server restart). The stories whose only point is a few args use the Playground's snippet builder, so "Show code" follows their controls; the others have hand-written snippets that now state every prop the story instance really has (its `defaultValue`, `placeholder` and limits).
- **A narrow box keeps everything inside (found by the user in Storybook):** below about 110px a chip's text (never wrapped or cut) and the entry's `8ch` minimum were wider than the box and left the border, by up to 29px and 40px at 90px. Each chip's text is now a label that ends in an ellipsis (the whole tag is in a tooltip, below), the tag may shrink, and its remove button stays in view; a chip that is cut shows its whole text in the DBM `Tooltip` on hover and when its remove button has focus (none for a chip that fits; the tooltip's trigger is the list item, because a trigger hands what it wraps a click handler and a `Tag` with one is a clickable tag, whose remove control is then only a decorative glyph, found by the unit suite going red); the entry's minimum is `min(8ch, 100%)`; the `+N more` and `Show less` buttons may shrink the same way. A hidden check at 5.5rem asserts every chip, button, entry and the clear-all button end inside the border and fails without the change.
- **`overflow: "wrap" | "collapse"` ([ADR-0051](../adr/0051-tagsinput-collapses-to-the-width-by-measuring-an-unseen-copy-of-its-chips-over-css-only-clipping-or-a-fixed-count.md)):** with `collapse`, an idle field is one line with as many chips as fit and a `+N more` button whose count follows the width; focus shows and wraps every tag as with `maxVisible`, which stays as an upper cap. The widths come from an unseen, `aria-hidden` copy of every chip read before paint and by a `ResizeObserver`; `fitCount` is the arithmetic (7 unit tests). Checked in Chromium by dragging the width both ways (the count goes up and down, always one line, everything counted), by focus opening and leaving collapsing again, by the cap, and by a row that fits showing no button; the width check fails without the measuring. jsdom checks the same with given widths.
- **Not done from the gap list:** locked or per-tag-toned tags (needs a richer value than `string[]`), asynchronous checks, editing a chip in place, suggestions, reordering.

**Deviations from the approved plan:**
- **List semantics:** the plan put the entry in the list as a `role="none"` item. axe failed it (`aria-required-children`). The list is now `display: contents` with only chips in it and the entry outside it, in the same flex-wrap box; axe passes and the list is announced as exactly as many items as tags (checked in Chromium). Not checked in Safari.
- **The entry is not detached from a surrounding form** (as `EditableText`'s open field is): Enter in an empty entry must submit the form, which implicit submission only does for a form owner. The entry has no `name` and no native `required`, so it contributes nothing to the data or the validation; the tags and the hidden validating control are what the form sees. A hidden story checks both Enter behaviours and that pressing a submit button reads pending text as a tag first.
- **Height:** the plan hoped for an empty field exactly as tall as an `Input`. A chip row is taller than `Input`'s line, so the block padding is the nearest token step per size, and an invisible zero-width chip keeps the row at chip height. Measured against an `Input` of the same size: xs +4.0px, sm +2.5px, md −0.6px, lg −2.7px, xl −3.9px; adding the first tag moves nothing.
- **Required validity:** the hidden validating control must not be read-only (a read-only control is barred from constraint validation); found by a failing real-browser check.
- **Targets:** the clear-all button had a 12px glyph box; it now has a `space.6` minimum.

**Known limits:**
- A refused typed-separator piece stays in the entry with the separator removed; a paste is added piece by piece and what is refused is counted, not itemized, in the announcement (the first reason is shown).
- The chips are not tab stops, so they are reached by an arrow key (named in the entry's description once there are tags) or in a screen reader's browse mode; a keyboard user who doesn't know the key won't find them by Tab.
- Collapsing with `maxVisible` unmounts the hidden chips, so a form that reads the DOM for chips sees only the visible ones; the hidden inputs hold every tag.
- `display: contents` on the list relies on the browser exposing the list role through it. Chromium and axe: yes. Safari with VoiceOver, and a real phone: not run.
- The chip row is as tall as a chip at every size; the field is within a few pixels of an `Input`, not equal to it.
- A tag can't be edited in place, only removed and re-added.
- No suggestions list: a later, additive layer from `Combobox`.

**Contrast (no new pairing):** the typed text is `text.primary` and the placeholder `text.tertiary` on `bg.surface` (13.53:1 and 8.21:1 in dark); the chips are `Tag`'s own, verified on `bg.surface`; the clear-all hover fill is `bg.neutral-subtle` under `icon.default`, `Input`'s own clear-button pairing.

**Tokens:** one new component token, `tags-input.entry-min-width` (`8ch`), the narrowest the typing area gets before it wraps beneath the chips; row added to `03-token-system-spec.md`. Nothing else new.

**Tests:** 87 unit tests, 8 for `fitCount` (structure and naming, placeholder only without tags, a non-array value, missing-name warning, Enter/separator/blur/paste adding, an empty Enter not swallowed and a pending one not submitting, separators and line breaks in a paste, trim and `transform` and a throwing `transform`, `addOnBlur` on and off and not leaving to a chip's button, IME Enter, refusing a repeat/`maxTags`/`validate` with messages and a throwing `validate`, a refused piece staying in the entry, `maxTagLength`, Backspace removal and announcement, text before tags, chip removal with focus, Escape clearing and not clearing, clear-all with a keyboard user's place, controlled tags and pending text wired to state, no mutation, read-only/disabled/error/required, phone-keyboard props, form submission and no pending text, `required` blocking and focusing the entry, ref/id/testid/className placement, `FormField` wiring, a box press focusing the entry, `labels` with `undefined`, `formatNumber`, spread order, server render, StrictMode, jest-axe in six states), a Docs-page token guard, and 22 hidden real-browser stories (height against `Input` at five sizes and no shift, wrapping in a narrow column with the entry's minimum width, text inserted at once and a pasted list, focus after removing, tab order, list semantics, 24px targets by pressing around each button, right-to-left, form values, `required` stopping a form, Enter in an empty entry, a submit press making pending text a tag, a phone width).

**Self-verification (real runs, 2026-10-10, after the final review below):** `pnpm lint` (eslint plus both typechecks) clean; unit project 6,887/6,887; Storybook (Chromium) project 1,336/1,336; `pnpm build` clean; `TagsInput` 6.41KB JS / 2.34KB CSS gzipped, within the 10KB budget; Foundations token coverage in sync; `pnpm check-guidelines` clean; the Docs page opened in a running Storybook (all template sections, 18 canvases, 44 Properties rows each described, 22 hand-written "Show code" panels, no error display).

## Final review before Finalization (2026-10-10)

A full §9 pass over the finished component. Findings, all fixed:

1. **An empty string in `separators` turned every character into a tag** (an empty pattern matches everywhere). Empty entries are ignored, with a development warning.
2. **`maxTagLength` didn't hold for a piece that arrives whole.** Typing is cut by the native `maxLength`; a paste, or text ended by a separator, bypassed it (a 54-character tag went in under a limit of 20, reproduced in Chromium). Such a piece is refused with `labels.tooLong`, announced like a duplicate, and counted in the paste summary; the others in the paste are kept.
3. **Shift, Ctrl, Meta or Alt with the left arrow at the start of the entry moved focus to a chip** instead of extending the selection or jumping a word. Only the plain key moves.
4. **Escape on a chip reached a surrounding handler**, unlike the entry's. It is stopped, as the entry's is while there is text to clear.
5. **Invalid combinations were silent.** `value` with `defaultValue`, `inputValue` with `defaultInputValue` and `showCount` without `maxTags` each warn once in development.
6. **Collapse mode was untested under `StrictMode` and on the server.** Both are now (the observer is live and the count right after a remount; `renderToString` doesn't throw).
7. **A paste went to the end of the typed text** whatever the caret. It goes in at the caret or over the selection, then everything is split.
8. **This file and the inventory row.** The test and size figures above; row 63 now cites ADR-0050 and ADR-0051 beside ADR-0049.

**Still not run:** purple dark and emerald light by eye, the breakpoint scale beyond a phone width, `overflow="collapse"` in right-to-left, a real operating-system clipboard with line breaks (the paste tests dispatch a clipboard event), Safari, a real phone, and a screen reader.

## Change after Finalization: the focus ring is drawn on the field's own edge (2026-10-11)
The same field-family rule as `Input`'s (see [Input.md](Input.md) and `05-component-api-conventions.md` §6): the ring sits on the box's own edge, the corners are not tightened while focused, and an invalid field keeps the ring in the danger colour. Found by the check: `.box:focus-within` out-ranked `.invalid`, so an invalid box's border went to the focus colour; the invalid rule now restores the danger border while focused. Covered by the shared real-browser check; the four themes and a real phone were not looked at live.

## Icons at the input's own step, 2026-10-19 (visual change; stays Finalized)

At explicit direction, following `Input`'s own change (see [Input](Input.md)): the icons this component draws in the field are the same step as its `size` (`xs` 12px … `xl` 32px) instead of one step below it. This is the clear-all icon; the chips and their remove icons keep their own sizes. A real-browser check in `Input.checks.stories.tsx` measures the icons at every size.
