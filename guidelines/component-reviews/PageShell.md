# PageShell — review findings

**Tier:** Template (a shell: it ships, [ADR-0179](../adr/0179-templates-ship-as-composable-shells-in-the-components-package-with-story-only-example-pages-over-shipping-finished-pages-or-keeping-all-templates-storybook-only.md)) · **Category:** Application shells · **Finalized:** not yet (the maintainer's to declare)

Second template, planned 2026-11-07 with `/plan-template` (its first real use) and approved by the maintainer with the recommendation on all nine decisions. Row 18 of the build order in `04-component-inventory.md` (the shell for an app with no sidebar; first named `TopNavShell`, renamed `PageShell` on 2026-11-07, [ADR-0186](../adr/0186-the-top-nav-shell-is-renamed-pageshell-over-keeping-topnavshell-or-renaming-appshell-as-well.md)).

## Decisions
- **Kind and place:** a shell, exported from the package, in `src/templates/PageShell/`, titled `Templates/Application shells/PageShell`. Its own small compound component, not `AppShell` without a sidebar and not a wrapper around it ([ADR-0185](../adr/0185-topnavshell-is-its-own-small-compound-shell-with-two-helpers-extracted-from-appshell-to-internal-over-appshell-without-a-sidebar-or-a-wrapper-around-it.md)).
- **Parts:** `PageShell` (root `div`), `.SkipLink`, `.Banner`, `.Header` (a `div`, or `as="header"`), `.SubNav`, `.Main` (`id="main"`, `busy`, `labels`), `.Content` (the `size`, `paddingInline` and `paddingBlock` of `AppShell.Content`) and `.Footer` (a `contentinfo`). No `State` part: an app draws an `EmptyState` in `Main`.
- **Root props:** `stickyHeader` (default `true`), `scrolledEdge` (default `true`), `pageKey` and `pageTitle`, `dir` (its own, else the nearest `DirectionProvider`'s), `className`, `style`, `id`, `data-testid`. No `labels` on the root: it draws no text; the skip link and the busy bar take their own. No controlled or uncontrolled pair: nothing is held that a caller would own. Every part is `forwardRef`, passes native props through, and puts `{...props}` before computed attributes.
- **The shell owns the sticky slot.** The page scrolls as a whole (no `scroll` prop); the header slot is `position: sticky` and holds the `Navbar` and an optional `SubNav` together; the `Navbar` inside must not set its own `sticky`, and `hideOnScroll` is out of v1. The root sets no `overflow` (it would stop `sticky` working).
- **The banner scrolls away** (not sticky), so the shell measures nothing and has no first-paint or hydration measuring cost.
- **`scrolledEdge`:** a shadow under the header while the page is scrolled (the existing `shadow` tokens), a line instead in forced colours.
- **`SubNav`:** a thin row inside the sticky slot that scrolls sideways when it overflows.
- **Footer** stays at the window's bottom on a short page (the root is at least `100dvh` tall, a column).
- **Print** as `AppShell` ([ADR-0183](../adr/0183-appshell-prints-the-page-without-its-navigation-chrome-over-printing-the-screen-layout-or-leaving-print-to-each-app.md)): the skip link is left out; the banner, header and footer are static and in order; nothing sticks; `main` prints in full. **Safe-area insets:** the header and the footer leave a notch or a home indicator clear.
- **Shared helpers extracted from `AppShell`** (the maintainer's go-ahead given with the plan): `usePageChange` and `MainBusy` go to `src/internal/`; `AppShell` imports them and its behaviour does not change, with its tests, hydration, `StrictMode` and print checks rerun.
- **Tokens:** all existing, none new (`bg.surface`, `text.primary`, `font-family.primary`, `border.default`, `border-width.1`/`2`, `border-focus`, `z-index.sticky`/`fixed`, the `shadow` pair, `space.*`, the motion tokens).
- **Responsive:** the viewport, not a container (it is the page frame). The `Navbar` already moves its links into a `Drawer` below its `collapseBelow`; the `SubNav` scrolls sideways at a phone; `Content`'s padding steps down. The page never scrolls sideways and the header never changes height as it sticks.
- **Demo:** a "Projects" workspace (a `Navbar`, a `Tabs` sub-navigation, cards and a long list, a footer of links), with demo controls for the content's length, `busy` and an empty or failed page. A right-to-left check, not a story.
- **States matrix:** loading (`busy`), empty and error (an `EmptyState` in `Main`; the error is announced once), a short page (the footer at the bottom), a long page (the header sticks, the shadow shows, `BackToTop` appears). Selection and a destructive confirm do not apply.
- **Landmarks:** one `banner`, the `Navbar`'s named navigation, the sub-navigation's own named navigation, one `main`, one `contentinfo`; one `h1` in the demo.
- **Checks planned**, each to be broken once to see it fail: the header stays at the top under a real wheel scroll (stickiness removed), the footer at the bottom on a short page (`min-height` removed), the skip link as the first tab stop, focus to `main` on a page change, the shadow while scrolled, and print; also no horizontal overflow at phone and tablet, forced colours, right to left (Persian, `lang="fa"`), hydration, `StrictMode`, and the snippets typechecked as real code (`pnpm check-snippets`, from its `PageShell.snippetScope.txt`).
- **Registrations:** a `07` §6 row, this file, the `04` row renamed and its status, a `componentDescriptions.ts` line, the per-component and the Storybook bundle-size runs, ADR-0185.

## Restructure plan (approved 2026-11-07, built one stage at a time with the maintainer's confirmation between stages)
**Rule behind it:** a template composes existing components; when one lacks something, that component is extended, never copied into the template. The shell as first built broke it: `SubNav`/`SubNavLink`, the header slot's sticking, shadow and hide-on-scroll, the skip link and the heading row duplicate what `Navbar` and the atoms already do.

**Decisions:** `PageShell` is renamed `PageShell` (`AppShell` keeps its name; both Docs open with "a sidebar means `AppShell`, none means `PageShell`"). The header is a real `Navbar` and the shell keeps only page-level parts (skip link, banner, `Body`, `Main` with `busy`, `Content` with `width`, `Aside`, `Footer`, page-change handling, `documentTitle`, safe-area and print rules). `Footer` is a plain slot: no `variant`, no `compact`, no `actions`. New components: a `SkipLink` atom (used by both shells and `Navbar.SkipLink`, which stays a thin wrapper with the same props) and a `PageHeader` molecule (compound: `Breadcrumbs`, `Title`, `Description`, `Meta`, `Actions`; `size`, `bordered`, `as`; actions wrap under the text on a phone by a container query; no landmark). `Navbar` gains `Navbar.Links scroll` (stays in the bar at every width, scrolls natively with edge fades and the current link revealed), a per-set `currentVariant` and `rounded`, and a trailing `sticky={false}` row, after a spike (stop and re-discuss if it fails: a template-only `SubNav` is not an acceptable fallback). `PageShell.Aside` is optional, a complementary landmark outside `<main>` inside a `PageShell.Body` (a `Container` plus grid), stacked under the page on a phone by default with an optional drawer (`PageShell.AsideToggle`), scrolls with the page and is optionally sticky under the header (`--page-shell-header-height`, as `AppShell` does); DOM order is the writer's, with no CSS reordering. `AppShell`'s aside drawer code moves to `src/internal/` at this second consumer.

**Stages, each its own commit(s), `/verify` at the end of each, nothing pushed without a go-ahead:** 0 process (the rule in `06` §9, both template commands, `04`'s naming rule); 1 rename and ADR-0186; 2 the `SkipLink` atom, adopted by `AppShell`, `PageShell` and `Navbar`; 3 the `Navbar` extension (ADR-0187; spike first, approval of the API before the build); 4 the shell onto `Navbar` and the removals (ADR-0188, superseding the "the shell owns the sticking" part of ADR-0185); 5 the `PageHeader` molecule, adopted by the shells' demos and the list page; 6 `PageShell.Aside` (ADR-0189); 7 review, Docs sweep, budgets and `/verify`. Finalized components (`Navbar`, `AppShell`, the list page) may be touched only as behaviour-preserving commits with their checks green, each confirmed.

**Not verifiable here:** Safari and Firefox (everything runs in Chromium), notably the banner landmark if any `display: contents` is used.

## Built (2026-11-07)
Built to the plan above. Three things were decided while building, each for a reason that showed up in the build:
- **`Content`'s `paddingInline` defaults to `4`**, not `AppShell.Content`'s `{ base: 4, md: 6, lg: 8 }`: a `Navbar` with `contained` pads its own content by `space.4`, so with the wider default the page's left edge sat 16px inside the bar's at a desktop width (measured: brand and sub-navigation at 16px, heading at 32px). The default is the step the bar uses, so the bar and the page share an edge.
- **The header's shadow is drawn only while the header sticks.** It was first drawn whenever the page was scrolled; a header that scrolls away (`stickyHeader={false}`) has nothing to mark, and the check for it found a shadow on a header that had left the window. The rule and its forced-colours twin are gated on `data-sticky-header`.
- **The loading strip is at the top of `<main>` and not stuck under the header** (`AppShell`'s is): the shell measures nothing, so there is no header height to stick it below, and a load starts at the top of a page.
- **`usePageChange` and `MainBusy` are in `src/internal/`** and `AppShell` uses them ([ADR-0185](../adr/0185-topnavshell-is-its-own-small-compound-shell-with-two-helpers-extracted-from-appshell-to-internal-over-appshell-without-a-sidebar-or-a-wrapper-around-it.md)); its 135 unit tests and 85 real-browser checks (hydration, `StrictMode`, print and busy among them) pass unchanged after the refactor.

## Additions after the first build (2026-11-07, at explicit direction)
Eleven items from a gap review, all built. The numbers are the review's priority order.
1. **The sub-navigation fades at an edge that has more links** (`data-overflow-start/end`, `useScrollEdges` over the row's links, a gradient that mirrors in right to left, never in the way of a click), has no visible scrollbar, and brings the current link into view when it mounts and when the current link changes (only its own box scrolls, never the page).
2. **`PageShell.SubNavLink`**: a round ghost `Button asChild` over an `<a>`, `current` giving `aria-current="page"` and the `Navbar`'s own current tint, underlined as well in forced colours. `SubNav` takes a `label` and draws the named `nav` itself; without one it draws none and a `nav` of your own goes in (the old way still works).
3. **Landmarks:** the `label` is what tells the sub-navigation's `nav` from the bar's and the footer's; the Docs and the unit test name the three.
4. **`stickyHeader="auto"`** hides the header on a scroll down past 80px and brings it back on a scroll up, at the top, and while focus or an open menu (`aria-expanded`) is inside it; a move under 6px is ignored; reduced motion jumps instead of sliding.
5. **`SubNav` `bordered`, `align` and `sticky`.** With `sticky={false}` the slot stops being a box (`display: contents`) and the bar, its first child, sticks on its own, with the shadow, the layer and the sliding moved to it. The first design (a negative `top` on the slot) put the bar off the window and a real-browser check caught it.
6. **`Content` `width`** (`reading`, `dashboard`, `full`) over `Container`'s steps; an explicit `size` wins.
7. **`documentTitle`** (`true` or a function of `pageTitle`), restored on unmount.
8. **`PageShell.PageHeader`**: title as the heading (`titleLevel`), `breadcrumbs`, `description`, `actions` (wrapping under the title on a phone). The demo page uses it.
9. **`Footer` `variant`** (`compact`, `actions`: a sticky bar over the page with a shadow and the bottom inset kept clear). A footer of columns is a `Grid` in the content, documented and not a variant.
10. **The skip link's stylesheet is shared** with `AppShell` in `src/internal/skipLink.module.css` (it was copied line for line). `AppShell` is Finalized: this is a refactor with no change in what it draws, and its unit tests and real-browser checks pass unchanged. The skip link gained the dark-theme shadow `AppShell`'s already had.
11. **Forced colours and print:** the current link underlines, the actions bar gets a line, the edge fades and any slide are removed for print.

**Checks added:** unit (15: auto-hide, the tab title, the sub-navigation's parts and its `data-subnav-static`, the footer and content variants, the page header) and real-browser (5: links centred, the current link's tint, the fade and the current link scrolled into view by a real click on a phone, a real wheel hiding and showing the header and focus keeping it, only the bar staying with `sticky={false}`, the actions footer at the window's bottom). The auto-hide and static-row checks were broken once and failed.

**Not decided here:** the 80px and 6px thresholds of the auto-hide are constants in the component, not props; a `SubNav` outside a shell now throws like the other parts that need it.

## Not verified
A real phone, Safari and Firefox (everything ran in Chromium, with the phone width emulated); a notched device (the insets were stood in through the custom properties); a screen reader's reading of the page-change announcement and the busy region; a real printer; the `Navbar`'s menu panel inside a Docs frame; a header taller than the window; the sub-navigation's native scrollbar, which shows as a thin line under the row on a phone.


## Stage 4: the shell leaves its header to `Navbar` and `Affix` (2026-11-07, approved by the maintainer, [ADR-0188](../adr/0188-pageshell-leaves-its-header-to-the-navbar-and-affix-and-keeps-only-page-level-parts-over-owning-the-header-slot-s-sticking-shadow-and-hiding.md))
- **Removed:** `PageShell.Header`, `.SubNav`, `.SubNavLink`; the root's `stickyHeader` and `scrolledEdge`; the scroll listener, shadow, hide-on-scroll and static-row logic; `Footer`'s `variant`. **Kept:** `SkipLink`, `Banner`, `Main`, `Content`, `PageHeader` (until it is a component), `Footer`, page-change handling, `documentTitle`, the frame, the side and bottom insets and print.
- **A custom header:** any element first in the shell. `Navbar sticky` sticks itself (shadow, `hideOnScroll`, a second row of links); a custom `<header>` sticks by `Affix` (`asChild`, a shadow from `onStickyChange`). **The `Affix` check** found no gap that needs a Finalized change: `Affix` sticks any element, takes `offset` and `scrollContainerRef`, and reports the stuck state; it has no hide-on-scroll (use `Navbar`) and no safe-area offset (the recipe below).
- **The slot did not survive:** the plan said `PageShell.Header` would stay as a thin placement slot, but a wrapper box breaks `Navbar sticky` (a sticky element sticks within its parent) and a trailing row that scrolls away needs the bar to be a direct child of the tall column, so the slot is gone and the header is a direct child of the shell.
- **The notch:** the root publishes `--page-shell-safe-top` (and left, right, bottom); a sticky header pads itself with it.
- **Checks kept and rewritten for a `Navbar sticky` header:** the widths without page overflow, a real wheel leaving the bar at the top, a header that does not stick, the banner scrolling away, the footer at the window's bottom, the skip link first and Enter reaching `main`, a click on a bar link, the loading strip, right to left, forced colours, print, the top inset. **Dropped, since `Navbar` owns them and has its own:** the shell's shadow, hide-on-scroll, sub-navigation, static row and actions footer.

**Stage 4 built (2026-11-07).** The shell is now only page-level: the root (direction, `pageKey`, `pageTitle`, `documentTitle`, the frame and the published insets), `SkipLink`, `Banner`, `Main`, `Content`, `PageHeader` and a plain `Footer`. The demo header is a sticky `Navbar` direct in the shell, with a trailing `Navbar.Row` of scrolling links for the second row, and a header of its own made sticky by `Affix` (a story and a snippet).
- **Unit (65):** the header a direct child of the shell with no box around it, a header of the app's own as the banner, the shell's own refs and passthrough, the page change, `busy`, `documentTitle`, the content and page header; the server render now asserts one banner and the trailing wrapper, and the hydration tests stub `IntersectionObserver` for the sticky bar.
- **Real browser (36):** the widths without page overflow (the phone's second row staying and scrolling), a real wheel leaving the bar at the top with its shadow while the row scrolls away, a `Navbar` without `sticky` scrolling away, the banner, the footer, the skip link and Enter, a click on a bar link, the loading strip, right to left, forced colours (the frame), the published insets (the bar pads itself with `--page-shell-safe-top`), print, a page that grows, and a header of the app's own sticking by `Affix` with its shadow. Broken once each (the `Affix` shadow, the bar's inset padding, the bar's `sticky`), four checks failed.
- **Regression:** 13,144 unit tests and 737 real-browser checks across the templates, `Navbar`, `Sidebar` and the atoms; snippets typecheck (15); `check-guidelines`.
- **Not yet:** `/review-template PageShell`, the `PageHeader` molecule and `PageShell.Aside` (stages 5 to 7); Safari and Firefox.

**Stage 5 (2026-11-07).** `PageShell.PageHeader` is removed: the head of a page is the [`PageHeader`](PageHeader.md) molecule, which the demo uses. The shell draws none of its own.
