# Menubar — review findings

**Status:** Finalized 2026-11-01, declared by the maintainer after the final review pass below. Built the same day with the `06-engineering-standards.md` §9 checklist applied as it was written. Open at finalization: the items under "Not verified" (a real touch device, a screen reader, Safari and Firefox, Windows high contrast on a real machine).

## What was built

A desktop-style bar of menus (File, Edit, View) for the commands of an application window. Compound (`Menubar.Menu`/`Trigger`/`Content`, with `Menu`'s item parts as `Item`/`CheckboxItem`/`RadioGroup`/`RadioItem`/`Label`/`Group`/`Separator`/`Sub`/`SubTrigger`/`SubContent`) over Radix `menubar` pinned at 1.1.24 on the shared layer stack. `size` (the shared scale, which also sets the menus' row size), `surface` (`ghost`, `outlined`, `filled`), `tone` (`brand` by default, or `neutral`), `loop`, `dir`, `value`/`defaultValue`/`onValueChange` and `onOpenChangeComplete(value, open)`.

## Decisions
- Pin, compound shape, the root holding the open menu's name, non-modal menus, a scrolling bar and no sheet: [ADR-0162](../adr/0162-menubar-wraps-radix-menubar-pinned-at-1-1-24-as-a-compound-of-named-menus-that-holds-the-open-menus-name-leaves-the-page-usable-and-scrolls-past-its-room-over-a-data-driven-menus-array-a-modal-bar-or-an-overflow-menu.md).
- The item parts, context and stylesheet moved to `internal/menu` first, in their own commit with no change to what `Menu` or `ContextMenu` draw: [ADR-0163](../adr/0163-the-menu-item-parts-context-and-stylesheet-move-to-internal-menu-at-the-third-consumer-over-menubar-importing-them-from-menu.md).
- No new token. A trigger is its padding plus a line of text (a `Button`'s approach) over a `space.6` floor.
- `tone` (added the same day, at direction) colours the triggers' hover and open fills and the bar's own border and fill, all from subtle tokens, with the trigger label `text.primary` in both tones: brand is `bg.brand-subtle` (hover, and the filled bar), `bg.brand-subtle-hover` (open, and hover on a filled bar) and `border.brand-subtle`; neutral is the `neutral` pairs. It does not change the menus the bar opens.
- `ArrowUp` on a trigger opens its menu onto the last item, as `Menu`'s trigger does; Radix Menubar handles `ArrowDown`, `Enter` and `Space` only.
- No `labels` and no `presentation`: the one `labels` entry the shared parts read is a sheet's "Back" row.
- Bundle: 6.2KB of JS and 2.6KB of CSS at first (it holds the shared item parts); 10.34KB and 2.84KB after the nine additions and the final review, so a 12KB JS budget (`JS_BUDGET_OVERRIDES_KB`). The shortcut formatting in the shared parts took `Menu` from 5.87KB to 6.31KB and `ContextMenu` from 7.02KB to 7.44KB.

## What was found while building it
- **A menu playing its exit closes the whole bar when another opens.** Radix Menubar closes by name, and the menu that is closing (the panel has an exit fade) hears the new menu's focus as focus outside it and calls `onOpenChange(false)`. A real pointer moving from File to Edit left both closed; jsdom has no animation and passed. The content ignores an outside interaction while its menu is not open. The real-browser check fails without the guard.
- **A trigger that is only partly in view is not scrolled to by the browser,** which acts on one that is wholly hidden, so the bar reveals the focused trigger itself, clear of its fade and its `scroll-padding`. The check arrows through every trigger of a bar a fifth the width it needs and fails without the reveal.
- **A press elsewhere must keep the focus it gave.** Radix returns focus to the trigger unless the close came from an outside press; the wrapper's pointer-close refocus (`focus({ focusVisible: false })`) skips that case too. The first version of the check clicked a field that sat under the open panel, which is a check bug, not a component one.
- **The deeper hover and open fills are not 3:1 against the focus ring in every theme** (neutral in Purple dark measured 2.43:1), so a trigger with keyboard focus rests on the bar's own fill and the ring is drawn on that. The check presses a real key, since `:focus-visible` never matches a synthetic one, and resolves its colour outside `waitFor` (a probe added on every poll hangs it).
- **A synthetic count of open menus races the exit animation.** Two panels exist for the length of the fade, so the check waits for one.
- **Radix's own props can replace the computed ones:** the bar's `role`, a trigger's `role`, `aria-haspopup` and `aria-expanded` and a panel's `role` and `aria-orientation` are restated after the spread; the probe fails without them.

## Nine additions (2026-11-01, at direction)
Decisions in [ADR-0164](../adr/0164-menubar-gets-a-controller-opt-in-hotkeys-an-action-part-disabled-reasons-platform-drawn-shortcut-hints-a-sticky-option-an-overflow-menu-and-a-trigger-icon-over-leaving-each-to-the-app.md): a `controllerRef` and `useMenubarController()`; opt-in `hotkeys`; `Menubar.Action`; `disabledReason` on a trigger and an action; shortcut hints written with `mod` drawn for the platform (in a menubar only); `sticky`, `stickyOffset` and `scrollContainerRef`; `icon` and `trailing`; `overflow="menu"` with `labels.more`; and a documented recipe for a phone (a `Menu` below a breakpoint).
- **Radix closes by name, so an action needed the bar's help.** Reaching an action by arrow key or pointer asks Radix to "open" it; the bar closes the open menu instead, focuses the action and tells the closing panel to leave focus there, or Radix hands it back to the menu's own trigger (the unit test and the real-browser check both fail without it).
- **A trigger with a reason must stay in Radix's collection,** so Radix is never told it is disabled; the bar refuses to open it by every route, including an arrow key inside another open menu (which leaves that menu open rather than skipping).
- **`overflow="menu"` is fed by each item registering itself.** The first build read the bar's children, so a component that returns menus was invisible to it (the first stories wrapped theirs in one and showed no More menu). Each `Menubar.Menu` and `Menubar.Action` now registers what it is and leaves a hidden marker for its place; a trimmed one renders only the marker. A transient render where newly registered items counted as hidden closed an open menu, so items that have just come are all drawn until measured. A bar is as wide as its items, so the room is the container's, read through a `ResizeObserver` on the parent.
- **An item with keyboard focus is held in the bar** until focus leaves (§9, a collapse never removes the control that has focus); the check fails without it.
- **A real hover on an action closes the open menu and the action keeps focus;** a real Alt+F opens File onto its first item; a trigger with a reason shows it on a real hover and on focus; a sticky bar stays at the top with a shadow; a `mod` hint is drawn for the platform in the bar and as written in a `Menu`.
- Two new hooks' rules: the React lint rule forbids changing a ref taken from a context, so the menu hands the trigger and panel a setter and a "take" function instead.
- Contrast: the icon (`icon.default`) is 5.1:1 or more on every fill the bar draws, in all four themes.
- Shortcut hints written with `mod` are drawn for the platform in `Menu` and `ContextMenu` too (at direction, [ADR-0164](../adr/0164-menubar-gets-a-controller-opt-in-hotkeys-an-action-part-disabled-reasons-platform-drawn-shortcut-hints-a-sticky-option-an-overflow-menu-and-a-trigger-icon-over-leaving-each-to-the-app.md)).
- **The Docs Playground panel can't edit an object,** so `hotkeys` and `labels` read "–" there, and `surface`, `tone` and `dir` are `select` controls, not `inline-radio`, which that panel draws as a text field. The Playground draws the bar in a box that can be narrowed, so `overflow` has a room to run out of.
- **Story controls were audited story by story:** each story keeps live only the controls its canvas shows (`onlyControls` in the stories file) and the rest read "–", `hotkeys` and `labels` (objects) read "–" everywhere, since they are set in code (the Hotkeys story carries a `hotkeys` arg), and the Playground box scrolls with text under the bar so `sticky` can be tried. **The bar now sets its own `dir` attribute** (Radix takes `dir` for its keys and does not put it on the element), so the `dir` control, and `dir="rtl"` alone, lay the triggers out from the right; the right-to-left story no longer needs a wrapper.
- **Every Docs snippet was type-checked against the real components** (a temporary harness compiled each one, the Playground's variants and every code example in the Docs page). The audit found a right-to-left snippet that omitted the `dir` container its story used, two icon comments missing, and a code-example block that mixed statements and JSX.
- Not built: a mnemonic underline on the trigger's letter, and a hotkey that opens a menu that has moved into More.

## Final review (2026-11-01)
A full pass of `06` §9 found nine things, all fixed:
- **`aria-label`, `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby` are declared on `Menubar.Trigger` and `Menubar.Action`** (they were only in the stories), and the reason's id is added to a described-by.
- **A bar with no accessible name warns once in development.**
- **`onSelect` is typed `(event: SyntheticEvent | Event)`:** an action that has moved into "More" gets the menu's own (native) event, which a cast had hidden.
- **Checkbox and radio items start over when their menu moves into "More"** (the content is drawn again there): tested both ways (controlled keeps its state) and said in the Docs and on `overflow`.
- **The focus hand-off to an action is forgotten when another menu opens**, so a stale note can't skip a later close's return of focus (the test guards the normal path; the stale path can't be reached from a test without a close that never reports).
- **The overflow measurement runs when its inputs change, not after every render;** the container's resize reports a new room (the unit test fires a resize observer).
- **Tests added:** `Menubar.Action`'s attributes and description, Home and End, a bar with no name, an action chosen from More, focus handed to an action, StrictMode with a hotkey, a controller, an action and the More registration, and a real mouse choosing an item in a menu hosted by a `Popover`.
- **`Menubar.tsx` was split** from 1,104 lines into `Menubar.tsx` (the root, 408 lines), `MenubarMenu.tsx`, `MenubarTrigger.tsx` (trigger and action), `MenubarContent.tsx`, `MenubarMoreMenu.tsx`, `useMenubarHotkeys.ts` and `useMenubarOverflow.ts`, with no change in behaviour (every test passed unchanged).
- **The numbers in this file and the budget comment were brought up to date.**

## Checks run
- 154 unit tests across four files (the first build's 28 were: rendering, ref and standard props, one tab stop, open and close by key and press, ArrowUp onto the last item, every arrow, wrapping, right-to-left, controlled and uncontrolled `value`, `onOpenChangeComplete`, every shared item part, a promise from `onSelect`, bar and content size, the attribute-precedence probe, a `Dialog` host, `StrictMode`, axe closed and open), plus server render and hydration of eight variants and the Docs-token test.
- 60 real-browser tests (Chromium) after the final review: the stories (visible, and hidden sub-part stories for the Properties tables, each with its axe run) and 26 checks, of which the first 14 were: an item chosen with a real mouse in a menu opened from inside a `Dialog`; the 24px target and no overflow at every size; the panel under its trigger's start edge; a real pointer moving to the next trigger; one tab stop from the neighbour; right-to-left; the overflowing bar; pointer close without a ring; contrast; forced colours. The hover check and the overflow check were broken on purpose once and failed.
- **Contrast**, text and the focus ring on every fill the bar draws (surface, neutral subtle, brand subtle), Purple and Emerald, light and dark, for both tones: text 6.59:1 or more on every fill it can sit on (`text.primary` on the deeper neutral fill, Purple dark, is the lowest) and the ring 3.56:1 or more on the resting fills, against floors of 4.5 and 3.
- Looked at live: the Docs page (sidebar position, tables, defaults) and the default theme and Emerald dark with a menu open.

## Not verified
- A real touch device, VoiceOver and a screen reader's announcement of the bar and its menus, Safari and Firefox (`focus({ focusVisible: false })` falls back to a ringed focus where unsupported), and Windows high contrast on a real machine (forced colours was emulated).
- Purple dark and Emerald light by a look (covered by the contrast measurements and the tokens).
- Known gaps, named and not built: an overflow menu for triggers that do not fit, and a sheet presentation for touch.
