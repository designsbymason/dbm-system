# Marketing Page — review findings

**Tier:** Template (a story-only example page, [ADR-0179](../adr/0179-templates-ship-as-composable-shells-in-the-components-package-with-story-only-example-pages-over-shipping-finished-pages-or-keeping-all-templates-storybook-only.md) and [ADR-0184](../adr/0184-a-story-only-template-page-is-a-folder-of-stories-and-a-story-kit-in-templates-with-demo-controls-and-no-playground-over-a-shipped-component-or-a-separate-pages-tree.md)) · **Category:** Data pages · **Finalized:** not yet (the maintainer's to declare)

Planned 2026-11-08 with `/plan-template`, approved by the maintainer with the recommendation on every decision; re-planned and re-approved 2026-11-11 with the five decisions below. It is the "Marketing or public page" row of `04-component-inventory.md`, built as a recipe: a page on `PageShell` that an app copies. It follows the [Documentation Page](DocumentationPage.md).

## Decisions
- **Kind and place:** a story-only example page in `src/templates/MarketingPage/`, titled `Templates/Data pages/Marketing Page`, tagged `example-page`, not exported.
- **Composition only:** `PageShell` with a `Navbar` whose links hold a `NavigationMenu` (a `Navbar.Links` already tells an embedded `NavigationMenu` how to look), `PageShell.Content` sections in three widths (a hero contained, features contained, a call-to-action band with `bleed`, a full-width strip with `width="full"`), `Grid` and `Card` for the features, a footer of columns of `Link`s in a `Grid` inside `PageShell.Footer`, and `BackToTop`. No part is drawn by hand beside a component that does it.
- **Demo:** a static product page (hero, three features, a proof strip, a call to action, a footer of four columns), with demo controls, not a Playground. No router; the copy is in one place.
- **Responsive:** the viewport. The header's panels are the `NavigationMenu`'s on a desktop and the `Navbar`'s menu panel on a phone; the features reflow from three columns to one; the footer's columns wrap. The page never scrolls sideways.
- **States matrix:** the page only (a marketing page is static). Selection and a destructive confirm do not apply.
- **Behaviour:** the `NavigationMenu`'s panels open by hover, click and keys, and `Escape` closes one and returns focus to its trigger; the skip link goes past the header to `main`; the phone menu opens and closes with the keyboard.
- **Landmarks:** one `banner`, the header's named `navigation`, one `main` with named `section`s where they matter, one `contentinfo` holding named `navigation`s for its columns; one `h1`.
- **Checks planned, each broken once to see it fail:** the landmark tree; the layout at phone, tablet and desktop widths with no page overflow; a `NavigationMenu` panel by a real click and real keys; the phone menu by real presses; the edge-to-edge bands (a `bleed` band's content lines up with the page); a hydration and `StrictMode` test; print; forced colours; the snippets typechecked as real code (from its `MarketingPage.snippetScope.txt`).
- **Registrations:** a `07` §6 row, this file, the `04` row (built as a page, with the "Marketing or public page" row marked as built as this recipe), and the `example-page` tag.

## Decisions approved 2026-11-11 (the re-plan)
- **Demo controls:** `features` (1 to 3 cards), `proof` (the strip or not) and `framed` (a Docs-page box). The page holds no state.
- **Shared helpers move to `src/templates/_shared/` now** (the second consumer, `05` §1): the skip link's click handler and the framed scroll box that `Navbar` and `BackToTop` take as `scrollContainerRef`. The Documentation Page's kit is changed to import them, with no change in behaviour; its checks must still pass.
- **Header:** a `Button` in `Navbar.Actions` on a desktop; on a phone it moves into the menu panel or stays by the button, whichever the preview shows fits.
- **Footer columns:** each a `nav` named by `aria-label`, with no heading, so the heading tree stays the hero's `h1` and the sections' `h2`s.
- **Menu:** one Product panel (`Feature`s and `Group`s) and two plain links.
- **Hero:** `Heading`, `Text` and `Button`s in the kit; no hero component.
- **No change to a Finalized component is planned**; one found while building is surfaced and confirmed first.

## Built (2026-11-11)
Built to the plan and the decisions above, in `src/templates/MarketingPage/`: a story kit (`MarketingPage`, with `features` and `proof` as its demo controls, and `framed` for the Docs page), two stories (the page, a short page), two typechecked snippets (the page's built from its controls), the Docs page, a stylesheet (`MarketingPage.module.css`, for the strip of figures) with its docs-token test, unit tests (6), a server render and hydration under `StrictMode` (3), and 10 real-browser checks.
- **Composition only:** `PageShell` (skip link, a sticky `Navbar` direct in it, `Main`, `Footer`), `NavigationMenu` in `Navbar.Links` (a Product panel with a `Group` and a `Feature`, and two plain links), `Navbar.Action hideBelow` with the same two buttons in `Navbar.PanelActions`, `PageShell.Content` in `reading`, `dashboard` and `full` and a `bleed` band, `Grid`, `Card`, `Icon`, `Stack`, `Stat`, `Container`, `Heading`, `Text`, `Button asChild`, `Link`, `Divider` and `BackToTop`. The only layout it writes is token-valued inline styles, one tint (`bg.brand-subtle`) and the figures' stylesheet.
- **Shared helpers in `src/templates/_shared/`** (the second consumer): `skipToMain` and `PageFrame` (the scrolling box and its `scrollContainerRef`), and, after the first review, `realInput.ts` (the real wheel, click, key and settle helpers of the real-browser checks, which five templates had each copied); the Documentation Page's kit imports the first two with no change in behaviour.
- **The figures:** a `Stack` of `Stat`s in a centred `Container` (no padding of its own, since the section pads), each `Stat` at `size="sm"` with no padding of its own and a class from the stylesheet: below `lg` it grows to share the row (`flex: 1 1 space.32`) with its content centred, so two to a row fill a phone (down to 320px); from `lg` it keeps that width with its content at the start and the row is `justify="between"`, so all four are on one row from a tablet up with equal gaps. The values are short ("12K+", "99.9%") because equal cells of `space.32` must hold the widest at 320px; `fitValue` was dropped because it shrinks only the long values, so the figures came out at different sizes.
- **Found while building it:**
  - **`PageShell.Content bleed` puts `style` on the band, not on its content**, so a `display: grid` there lays the band's one inner container out, not the heading and the button. The content's layout is a `div` inside the band; the Docs page and the snippet say so. Not a defect (the prop's JSDoc says `style` tints the band).
  - **The card header spreads its children along the row**, so an icon and a title written side by side sat at opposite ends: they are one group in a `div`.
  - **Print:** the `Navbar` stays `position: sticky` in print (it is in the flow there and does not repeat), and `BackToTop` is fixed but transparent until the page scrolls, so the print check asserts what is true (the header prints once at the top; nothing visible is fixed) rather than the plan's "nothing sticky". A print rule on the `Navbar` is the maintainer's call; none was added.
  - **A hero heading at `4xl` filled a 375px window edge to edge**; it is `3xl`.
  - **A real pointer left over the bar made a click close the menu it opened** in a full real-browser run (the primitive's hover timer, 200ms): the mega-menu check parks the pointer first and waits longer than a second for what a press changes (`slow`).
- **Checks, each broken once and failed:** `bleed` (removed), the features' `lg` column count (3 to 2), the footer's phone columns (2 to 1), the stat padding (back), the centring (removed), print (a visible fixed element), forced colours (the band opted out of adjustment), the menu's Escape (ignored), the footer links' tab order (taken out), the tablet (the bar collapsing at `lg`), and the band's content width (not the hero's). The real-browser checks: each landmark once, the cards in one row and the figures equal and spread at a desktop; the band edge to edge with its content on the hero's edges, the figures full width and the hero centred; a real click, `Enter` and `Space` on Product opening its panel and `Escape` returning focus to it; the keyboard path in markup order and the skip link's `Enter` reaching `main`; the footer ending the page and a real wheel bringing up `BackToTop`; the tablet (two columns, four figures on one row, the links kept in the bar); the phone (one column, two footer columns, two figures to a row filling the width and centred, one menu button, a panel with the links and both buttons, `Escape` and focus back, and `Enter` on the button); print; forced colours.
- **Looked at** in the browser pane at 1280, 1024, 820, 768, 375 and 320px (320 for overflow), in purple dark and emerald light, the Product panel opened by a real click, and the Docs page with both "Show code" blocks opened.
- **Not verified:** a real phone, Safari and Firefox, a screen reader, a real printer, and right to left (a page needs none).

## Review pass 1 (2026-11-11)
Seven findings, fixed at the maintainer's direction; not Finalized.
- **This file was out of date** (no stylesheet, the old figures layout, the earlier checks): the Built section above is rewritten.
- **The real-input helpers were copied between five templates' checks:** `realClick`, `pressKey`, `pressEscape`, `wheel`, `settled` and `nextFrame` are in `_shared/realInput.ts` and the checks of `AppShell`, `PageShell`, `DataTableListPage`, `DocumentationPage` and `MarketingPage` import the ones that were identical (`PageShell`'s own `realClick` differs and stays). `pressKey` takes `Tab`, `Enter`, `Space` and `ArrowDown`.
- **The planned check that the band's content lines up with the page is back:** the call to action's content has the hero's left edge and width.
- **Five checks that had never failed were each broken once and failed** (print, forced colours, the menu, the keyboard path, the tablet), with the band's alignment.
- **A stale comment** in the kit (the figures' text "left-aligned") now says centred below `lg` and at the start from it.
- **The call to action's tint in dark mode** is `bg.brand-subtle` (`#24222a` over the page's `#2c2a34`, a recess where light mode is a faint tint): kept, since the token is meant to be subtle and the band is told apart by its spacing and layout; its text measures 15.05:1 (headings), 11.65:1 (secondary) in dark and 13.46:1 and 6.56:1 in light, in both brands. A stronger tint needs a different semantic token (a decision for `03`), not a hover token.
- **A claim the checks did not prove:** the Docs said `Space` and the Down arrow open the Product panel and the phone menu opens with the keyboard. `Space` and `Enter` are now pressed for real on both, and the Down arrow is **not true here**: with the pointer parked, a real Down arrow on the closed Product trigger moves focus to the next link (Pricing) and does not open it, so the claim is taken out of this page's Docs. **`NavigationMenu`'s own Docs say the Down arrow opens a panel** (`NavigationMenu.mdx:170`); that is a Finalized component, so it is brought to the maintainer to decide (correct the sentence, or make the Down arrow open a closed trigger in a horizontal menu), not touched here.
- **Show code and the code examples** were read against the page: the page's snippet is built from its controls (cards and strip), shows all four figures, both hero buttons and all four footer columns, and says where `styles.figure` comes from; the Docs page's code examples have the strip and its stylesheet.
