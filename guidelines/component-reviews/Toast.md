# Toast — review findings

Sixth organism, built 2026-10-12 with the `06-engineering-standards.md` §9 checklist applied as it was written. **Finalized 2026-10-13**, after the full review pass (`/review-component Toast`) and its fixes, with the items under "Open at Finalization" accepted as open: none changes the component. What follows is what the build found and checked, then the follow-ups and the review.

## Decisions
- Wraps Radix Toast, pinned exactly at 1.2.23 so the layer stack stays one copy: [ADR-0069](../adr/0069-toast-wraps-radix-toast-pinned-at-1-2-23-on-the-shared-layer-stack-over-a-hand-rolled-toast-or-the-newest-release.md).
- Raised from code through a provider and a hook, with the declarative parts beside it, a `limit` and a queue, logical placement and a required `altText`: [ADR-0070](../adr/0070-toast-is-raised-from-code-through-a-provider-and-a-hook-with-a-declarative-toast-beside-it-over-a-module-level-function-or-component-only-use.md).
- Draws with `Alert`'s tone, variant and size colours (the pairings were contrast-checked there), its own dismiss button ([ADR-0008](../adr/0008-closebutton-reserved-for-modal-surfaces.md)) and `Alert.Action`'s approach to the action's colours. One new token, `toast.width`.

## What was found while building it
- **Radix writes `pointer-events` inline on a toast, and turns it off for every layer below an open modal.** A toast already showing when a dialog opened was drawn above the scrim and did nothing when pressed; one raised afterwards worked. The toast's own rule is `pointer-events: auto !important`, with the reason beside it, and a real-browser check presses each with a real mouse.
- **Radix spreads a caller's props after writing `data-state`, `data-swipe-direction` and `tabindex`,** so a same-named prop replaced them (a `data-state` of the caller's would stop an exit from ever ending). They are written again after the spread (`05` §3), with a test.
- **A modal dialog hides the toasts' region from assistive technology** (it hides the rest of the page), so a toast's action can't be reached by a screen reader or the keyboard while a modal is open. The announcement is made separately and is unaffected, `altText` is required for this reason, and the Docs page says so.
- **With focus in a modal dialog, `Escape` closes the newest toast first and the dialog on the next press.** That is Radix's layer order, kept and documented; a real-browser check pins it.
- **jsdom has no animations,** so a closing toast's exit completes at once there; the unit tests settle it explicitly and the real-browser checks wait for the animations to finish before measuring (§9).
- **The announcement text goes into the live region a frame after it is in the page,** which is what makes it a change; the tests wait for it rather than reading it at once.

## Follow-ups built after the first pass (2026-10-12)
The seven gaps named after the build, decided in [ADR-0071](../adr/0071-toast-actions-may-take-a-promise-a-repeated-or-replaced-toast-is-drawn-afresh-and-clear-all-is-a-toast-of-its-own-over-a-plain-button-or-a-timer-nudge.md): an action that takes a promise, a repeated or replaced toast drawn afresh (its time restarts and it is read again), the gap closed when one leaves, "Clear all", merged identical messages, `createToaster()`, and region-wide pausing confirmed and documented.
- **A promise action marked its reason at the press, so a later timeout read as the action** and slipped past the busy hold. The press now takes its mark back when a promise is returned.
- **`Button` writes `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled` itself, after the props it is given,** so busy is set on the element; a unit test and a real-browser check read it back.
- **A plain "Clear all" button among the list's children was skipped by Radix's Tab handling** (it moves only among its own items), so F8 then Tab never reached it. It is a toast of its own to Radix, and a real-browser check tabs to it from F8 and presses Enter.
- **A merge needs to know the id was made up here:** the id is stored on the entry, so every toast looked as if the caller had given one and nothing merged until the entry recorded which it was.
- **Collapsing needs the height before the exit begins:** read at the moment of closing it was too late for the animation's first frame, so it is kept as a custom property while the toast is open.
- **Checks added:** 18 unit tests (a promise action holding, not timing out and closing with `action`, a rejection to `onError`, merging and what does not merge, `formatNumber` and `labels.repeated`, a replaced toast's time and announcement, Clear all, `createToaster()`) and 7 real-browser checks (neighbours gliding rather than jumping, a pointer on one toast holding all, the count, Clear all by keyboard, a promise action holding past the toast's time, a rejection surfacing as an unhandled rejection with the toast kept, and a toaster raising before any provider).

## Controls and tables audited (2026-10-13)
- **The Playground now has a control for every prop it can show:** `icon`, `id`, `actionVariant` (while an action is on), `clearAll`, `mergeDuplicates` and `hotkey` were missing; `duration`'s "until dismissed", `role`'s "Default" and the icons are Playground-only choices that map to the real values, so they stay out of the meta-level argTypes. Each was driven live in a browser and its effect on the canvas read back. Props that take a function, an object or an element (`labels`, `formatNumber`, `toaster`, `container`, the callbacks) have no control and are in the tables.
- **`Toast.Action`'s table lacked `Button`'s own props** (`leadingIcon`, `trailingIcon`, `rounded`, `fullWidth`, `isLoading`, `loadingText`, `type`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`); it lists them now, as `Alert.Action`'s does.
- **`id`, `className`, `style` and `data-testid` close every story's Controls panel as non-interactive rows ("–"),** as on the other components; the toast's own identifier is `toastId` there, so the two don't share a row.
- **Order:** the Controls panel and the Playground read what it says, how it looks, what it does, then the provider's settings and the callbacks; each Properties table reads content, visual, behaviour, then the escape hatches last.
- **Merging was blocked by the Playground's own callbacks:** a callback made two calls differ, so a Playground that logs `onOpenChange` could never merge. Functions are now compared by identity, so a handler declared once and reused still merges ([ADR-0071](../adr/0071-toast-actions-may-take-a-promise-a-repeated-or-replaced-toast-is-drawn-afresh-and-clear-all-is-a-toast-of-its-own-over-a-plain-button-or-a-timer-nudge.md), amended).

## The fanned stack (2026-10-13)
`stack="fan"`, decided in [ADR-0072](../adr/0072-toast-gets-an-optional-fanned-stack-that-expands-on-pointer-or-focus-over-replacing-the-list-or-a-toggle-button.md): the newest toast in full at the edge, older ones pulled onto it by `translate` and `scale` (so the entrance, swipe and exit `transform`s are untouched), spread into the list while the pointer is over the toasts or focus is inside them, folded back a motion duration after both go; "Clear all" only while spread out.
- **The list is drawn through a portal after the first render,** so the layout hook found no element and never ran; the list is held as state for it.
- **The toasts' events don't pass through the list's React props,** since they are portaled into it: `onPointerEnter` and `onFocus` on the list never heard a toast. The pointer and focus are heard with native listeners on the list. The same was true of the focus bookkeeping for returning focus, which had worked only because the region's own focus (F8) happens on the list itself; it is native too now.
- **A toast leaving collapses its room while the others are positioned from where they are,** so a stale offset made the older toasts jump by the leaver's height and glide back. The layout is measured on every frame while one is leaving, with the transitions off, and a real-browser check asserts no frame moves a toast by anything like a toast's height.
- **The real pointer outlives a story,** so a fan spread out under where an earlier check left it; each fan check puts the pointer on the page's own button first.
- **Checks added:** 8 unit tests (the prop and a responsive map, depth and layer values, spread on the pointer and on focus, folding back, moving between toasts, Clear all only spread out, the list unchanged, document and tab order) and 6 real-browser checks (collapsed offsets and widths measured against the front toast, a real pointer spreading it into the list and back, focus and F8 with Tab reaching every toast, the top placement growing the other way, taking one from the middle without a jump, the list left as it was).
- **Not verified:** a fan on a real phone, with a finger swiping the front toast over the others.

## List order and the "Clear all" button (2026-10-13)
[ADR-0073](../adr/0073-toast-list-puts-the-newest-on-top-and-clear-all-at-the-bottom-at-every-edge-over-the-newest-against-the-edge.md): in the list the newest toast is at the top and "Clear all" at the bottom at every placement (a fan keeps its newest in front, at the edge). "Clear all" is a ghost button with no border or shadow, on the page's surface so its label stays readable, with less padding and the small button's label size, still 24 by 24.
- **Where a list grows from decides which way its neighbours move:** against the top edge a leaving toast lets the ones below it move up; against the bottom edge the ones above it move down to the edge, and an arriving toast is added above without moving the rest. The glide check runs at the top placement and a separate check pins the bottom list's order and the position of "Clear all".
- **At the bottom edge a new toast is added above the others, and a toast that leaves lets the ones above it glide down,** which is what the bottom list does as built; a real-browser check now pins it. It also found that "Clear all" arriving below the oldest (with the second toast) shoved the first toast up by its height at once; it now opens that room over a motion duration, and a check asserts the first toast glides rather than jumps.
- **A ghost `Button` has a tinted fill in this system, not none,** so the check compares the bar's button against a real ghost button's colours rather than against transparent.
- **Checks:** a real-browser check for the order and "Clear all" at the bottom edge, one for the ghost look (colours, label size, little padding, 24 by 24, no border or shadow on the bar), and the glide and list checks adjusted to the new order.

## Final review pass (2026-10-13)
Found by a read-and-verify pass over every part, run and fixed the same day.
- **`Toast.Action asChild` threw** (`Slot failed to slot onto its children`): the button's children were a spinner slot and the label, which is two nodes whenever `asChild` is set. In that mode only the label is rendered, and busy is `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled` on the element. Covered by two unit tests (a link that closes the toast, and a promise action that holds it) and a real-browser check with a real press.
- **Focus fell to the page when "Clear all" went while it had focus** (a toast dismissed from code with the keyboard on the bar): it moves to the toast that is left, and to the list when none is. A unit test and a real-browser check cover it, and one that focus elsewhere is left alone.
- **The repeat count was text in an icon colour** (`icon.*`), at 4.51:1 on the info and neutral subtle toasts in both light themes; it is `text.secondary` now (`--toast-text`, the on-fill colour on a solid toast). All four themes were measured from the resolved tokens: the lowest text pairing is 4.5 or more, and the dark themes 5.86.
- **Radix prop audit, decided:** `swipeThreshold` is exposed on the provider (50 px by default; a control in the Playground, a row in the table, a snippet). `announcerContainer`, `onSwipeStart`/`Move`/`Cancel`/`End`, `onPause`/`onResume` and `forceMount` are audited and left out: the announcements are made in the page's body whatever the `container`, which is where they have to be to be heard above a modal, and the swipe and pause callbacks have no use that `onOpenChange`'s `reason` does not already serve.
- **The warning for a missing provider** is once per component that calls `useToast()` there, not once; the comment says so.

## Open at Finalization
Accepted as open; none changes the component.
- **Not verified:** a real finger on a touch screen (the swipe and the fan are driven by a real mouse through the Chrome DevTools Protocol), a screen reader reading the announcements, and Safari. Only Chromium ran.
- **The four themes were measured, not looked at one by one:** contrast was computed from the resolved tokens in all four (the lowest text pairing is 4.5 or more, 5.86 in the dark themes), and the toast and the fan were looked at in one theme.
- **Forced colours** is asserted by a real-browser check (a system-colour border on every toast, none left to a shadow) and was not looked at.
- **"Clear all" is read out when it appears** ("Notification, Clear all"), which is Radix always reading a toast's label.
- **An action's size follows the toast's** and cannot be set on its own, as `Alert.Action`'s does.
