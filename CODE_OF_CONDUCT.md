# Code of conduct

DBM System is a place for people to talk about building accessible interfaces. Everyone who takes part is expected to be respectful, and to help keep it welcoming for people of every background and ability.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep issues and comments on topic, specific and constructive.
- Welcome questions and give feedback that helps the other person improve their report or idea.
- Respect that this is a solo-maintained project with limited time, and that nobody owes you a reply or a fix.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated demands for a response.
- Anything else a reasonable person would think inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, the Storybook and the documentation site, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting form described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and say that it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared.

The maintainer decides what is appropriate and may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository. A first minor problem usually gets a private request to stop; serious or repeated problems may be a block without warning.

## Attribution

This policy is written for this project and follows the spirit of widely used community codes of conduct.
